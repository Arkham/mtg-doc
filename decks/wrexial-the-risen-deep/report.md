## Strategy

Build Wrexial as optimized Dimir control-combo: use efficient interaction and premium draw engines to establish control, then exploit stocked opposing graveyards through Wrexial. Replace expensive theft and standalone mill with wheel disruption, compact Mindcrank finishes, and Hullbreaker Horror mana loops, retaining Exsanguinate and Blue Sun's Zenith as outlets. This pushes power within the supplied Bracket 4 rules and the 15-swap limit; reserve wins and game-ending wheel locks for turn four or later rather than pursuing an early cEDH race.

**Themes:** Dimir control, Graveyard theft, Wheel disruption, Mindcrank combos, Artifact mana loops, Tutor-supported finishers

## Synergies

- **Unblockable, Immediate Wrexial** (Wrexial, the Risen Deep + Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth + Lightning Greaves): Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth makes every opponent's lands Swamps, enabling Wrexial's swampwalk against anyone who controls a land. Lightning Greaves adds haste and shroud, letting Wrexial attack immediately while protected from targeted removal.
- **Graveyards Become Your Toolbox** (Wrexial, the Risen Deep + Dreamborn Muse + Drown in the Loch + Animate Dead): Dreamborn Muse stocks opponents' graveyards with spells for Wrexial and creatures for Animate Dead. Those growing graveyards also expand the range of spells and creatures Drown in the Loch can answer.
- **Cipher-Powered Hand Suppression** (Wrexial, the Risen Deep + Whispering Madness + Narset, Parter of Veils): Encode Whispering Madness on Wrexial to turn his evasive combat hits into repeated wheels, while Narset, Parter of Veils limits each opponent to at most one card drawn that turn. The discarded cards stock future Wrexial attacks; newly discarded spells cannot become targets of a Wrexial trigger already on the stack.
- **Two Krakens, Twice the Theft** (Wrexial, the Risen Deep + Sakashima the Impostor): Sakashima the Impostor can copy Wrexial while retaining its own name, allowing both to coexist despite the legend rule. Each has islandwalk, swampwalk, and its own combat-damage spell-theft trigger.
- **Steal the Entire Windfall** (Notion Thief + Windfall): With Notion Thief on the battlefield, Windfall discards everyone's hands but redirects all opponents' Windfall draws to you. This creates a massive hand advantage while filling opposing graveyards for Wrexial.
- **Combat and Drain Become Mill** (Wrexial, the Risen Deep + Mindcrank + Exsanguinate): Mindcrank turns Wrexial's combat damage into milling and makes Exsanguinate mill every opponent for the life they lose. This supplies more targets for future Wrexial triggers, while Exsanguinate also replenishes your life total.
- **Reusable Board Reset** (Academy Ruins + Oblivion Stone): After sacrificing Oblivion Stone to clear the board, Academy Ruins puts it back on top of your library for another use. If time and mana permit, a fate counter can preserve Wrexial through the destruction.
- **Recharge the Big Mana Rocks** (Tezzeret the Seeker + Mana Vault + Basalt Monolith): Tezzeret the Seeker's +1 untaps Mana Vault and Basalt Monolith without paying their normal untap costs. Untapping both after tapping them produces another six colorless mana, accelerating Wrexial, expensive interaction, or a large Exsanguinate.
- **Multiply the Draw Engine** (Consecrated Sphinx + Rite of Replication): A kicked Rite of Replication targeting Consecrated Sphinx creates five additional Sphinxes. With the original still present, each card an opponent draws can yield twelve cards for you.
- **Upkeep Mill Becomes Life Drain** (Bloodchief Ascension + Dreamborn Muse): Once Bloodchief Ascension has three quest counters, every card Dreamborn Muse mills from an opponent can make that opponent lose 2 life and you gain 2 life. This turns the deck's graveyard-filling engine into a substantial multiplayer finisher without requiring an infinite combo.

- **Dedicated mill/combo:** Use repeatable mill and library-halving effects, with **Duskmantle Guildmage**, **Bruvac the Grandiloquent**, or **Fraying Sanity** as finishers. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/wrexial-the-risen-deep/mill))

([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/wrexial-the-risen-deep))

## 3. Popular combos—exact outputs

These appear on Wrexial’s EDHREC combo page; **none requires Wrexial himself**. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/combos/wrexial-the-risen-deep))

- **Duskmantle Guildmage + Mindcrank:** Activate the Guildmage’s first ability for **{1}{U}{B}**, then cause an opponent to lose life or put a card into their graveyard. Mill causes life loss, which causes more mill. **Result: a lethal life-loss/mill loop**, provided the opponent has enough cards to sustain it; seed each opponent to eliminate the table. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/936-5069/))
- **Bruvac the Grandiloquent + Maddening Cacophony:** Cast **Maddening Cacophony** kicked, costing **{4}{U}{U}** total. Bruvac doubles the rounded-up half-library mill. **Result: every opponent mills their entire library**, absent replacement effects or other interference—not literally an infinite loop. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/95-5080/))
- **Fraying Sanity + Maddening Cacophony:** A kicked Cacophony mills half of every opponent’s library; at the end step, **Fraying Sanity** mills the enchanted opponent’s remainder. **Result: one opponent’s library emptied**, with the others approximately halved. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/combos/mono-blue/464-5080))
- **Fraying Sanity + Traumatize:** **Result: the enchanted opponent mills their whole library if its starting size is even; ordinarily one card remains if odd**, unless another card entered their graveyard that turn. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/combos/mono-blue/464-781))

## 4. Common mistakes / underperformers

*These are interaction-based cautions and deckbuilding judgments, not a measured community “worst cards” ranking.*

- **The Mindskinner is a major nonbo:** It prevents your creatures’ damage to opponents, so Wrexial does **not** deal combat damage and does **not** trigger spell theft. It belongs in a committed mill plan, not automatically in a Wrexial-value deck. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/648-6003/))
- **Too much small, single-opponent mill/discard:** **Glimpse the Unthinkable**, standalone **Traumatize**, and **Mind Rot** need sufficient payoffs; otherwise they spend cards without controlling the board. EDHREC’s discard article explicitly flags Mind Rot’s poor multiplayer scaling. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/articles/singleton-shmingleton-build-a-theft-deck-with-mind-rot))
- **Overloading expensive sea monsters:** My assessment: **Fleet Swallower** and **Stormtide Leviathan** are defensible themed finishers, but too many such cards compete with casting—and recasting—Wrexial. Keep early ramp and interaction intact. ([mtggoldfish.com](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/7182936))
