### How to play

This deck ramps into Wrexial, the Risen Deep and casts spells from opponents’ graveyards for free. It closes with commander damage, stolen threats, or a big Exsanguinate.

**Early game**

- Use Sol Ring to cast Dimir Signet, which supplies both colors for your commander.
- Find Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth with Expedition Map to make every opponent vulnerable to swampwalk.
- Play Baleful Strix to draw a card and discourage early attackers.

**Mid game**

- Give Wrexial, the Risen Deep haste with Lightning Greaves to get a spell right away.
- Send Wrexial, the Risen Deep at the opponent whose graveyard has the best spell.
- Copy Wrexial, the Risen Deep with Sakashima the Impostor to keep both and double up.

**Late game**

- Untap Mana Vault and Thran Dynamo with Tezzeret the Seeker for a bigger Exsanguinate.
- Pair Exsanguinate with Mindcrank to stock surviving opponents’ graveyards with more spells.
- Use Blatant Thievery to turn each opponent’s best permanent against them.

**How it wins**

- Finish low-life opponents together with a big Exsanguinate.
- Land four unblocked hits with Wrexial, the Risen Deep to eliminate an opponent.
- Win through combat with the strongest creature you can steal using Bribery.

**Key cards**

- Wrexial, the Risen Deep: Turns combat damage into free spells from the defending player’s graveyard.
- Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth: Turns every land into a Swamp so swampwalk works.
- Sakashima the Impostor: Copies your commander without making you lose either creature.
- Tezzeret the Seeker: Finds mana rocks and untaps them for big turns.
- Exsanguinate: Turns spare mana into table-wide life loss and keeps you alive.

**Mulligan:** Keep three lands, access to both colors, and early acceleration like Sol Ring or Dimir Signet.

**Watch out for**

- Graveyard exile can leave Wrexial, the Risen Deep without a spell to cast.
- Opponents can remove Wrexial, the Risen Deep in response to equipping Lightning Greaves.
- Hits from Sakashima the Impostor don’t count toward commander damage.

### Synergies

- **Swampwalk on Demand** (Wrexial, the Risen Deep + Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth + Expedition Map): Expedition Map finds Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth, which makes every land a Swamp in addition to its other types. That lets Wrexial, the Risen Deep attack any opponent who controls a land without being blocked, so you can pick the graveyard with the best spell.
- **Two Kraken, Two Spells** (Wrexial, the Risen Deep + Sakashima the Impostor + Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth): Sakashima the Impostor can copy Wrexial, the Risen Deep while keeping its own name, so the legend rule lets you keep both. With Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth enabling both creatures’ swampwalk, they can hit different players or take two distinct spells from the same graveyard.
- **Drain the Table, Stock the Graves** (Exsanguinate + Mindcrank + Wrexial, the Risen Deep): Exsanguinate makes each opponent lose life, and Mindcrank turns that loss into an equal number of milled cards for each of them. Combat damage from Wrexial, the Risen Deep also triggers Mindcrank; the newly milled cards cannot be chosen for that same hit, but they can supply targets for later attacks.
- **Artifact Ramp on Repeat** (Tezzeret the Seeker + Mana Vault + Thran Dynamo + Exsanguinate): Tezzeret the Seeker can fetch either mana rock or untap both Mana Vault and Thran Dynamo, getting another six colorless mana without paying the usual untap cost for Mana Vault. ### Strategy

Play Dimir control: use cheap interaction and tutors to set up a finish, and deploy Wrexial, the Risen Deep when you can connect with an opponent whose graveyard has a spell worth stealing. Windfall stocks those graveyards or strips opposing hands alongside Notion Thief or Narset, Parter of Veils, so the deck doesn't need dedicated mill spells that do little on their own. Finish with Mindcrank and either Bloodchief Ascension or Duskmantle Guildmage, or turn Hullbreaker Horror and cheap mana rocks into enough mana for a lethal Exsanguinate. This is a high-power Bracket 4 control deck that expects at least four turns of play rather than racing to win in the opening turns.

### How to play the suggested deck

Wrexial, the Risen Deep attacks through islandwalk or swampwalk to cast spells from opponents’ graveyards. Close with a Mindcrank loop, a big Exsanguinate, or Thassa's Oracle after milling yourself.

**Early game**

- Lead with Sol Ring into Dimir Signet to accelerate and fix your colors.
- Use Expedition Map to find Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth and open up attacks.
- Land Mesmeric Orb early to stock opposing graveyards for Wrexial, the Risen Deep.

**Mid game**

- Equip Lightning Greaves to attack immediately with Wrexial, the Risen Deep.
- Swing Wrexial, the Risen Deep at the player whose graveyard offers the best spell.
- Cast Windfall with Narset, Parter of Veils out to leave opponents short on cards.

**Late game**

- Use Tezzeret the Seeker to untap Mana Vault and Thran Dynamo before casting Exsanguinate.
- Use Bolas's Citadel and Sensei's Divining Top to draw repeatedly for one life per card.
- Hold Fierce Guardianship to protect Thassa's Oracle while your commander is out.

**How it wins**

- With Mindcrank and charged Bloodchief Ascension, make each opponent lose life to start their loop.
- With Mindcrank out, activate both Duskmantle Guildmage abilities to start a lethal loop.
- Tap and untap Basalt Monolith repeatedly with Mesmeric Orb, then cast Thassa's Oracle.

**Key cards**

- Wrexial, the Risen Deep: Turns combat damage into free spells from opposing graveyards.
- Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth: Makes swampwalk work against anyone who controls a land.
- Mindcrank: Turns life loss into mill and powers two finishing loops.
- Mesmeric Orb: Stocks opposing graveyards and enables your self-mill finish.

**Mulligan:** Keep two or three lands with blue and black access and early acceleration like Dimir Signet or Sol Ring.

**Watch out for**

- Save Force of Will for graveyard exile that would blank Wrexial, the Risen Deep.
- Cast Thassa's Oracle before your next draw after milling yourself with Mesmeric Orb.
- Pair Hullbreaker Horror and Sol Ring with a zero-cost artifact like unkicked Everflowing Chalice.

Those same cards raise the mana value ceiling for both modes of Drown in the Loch.
- **Discard Now, Cast Later** (Windfall + Narset, Parter of Veils + Wrexial, the Risen Deep): Windfall makes everyone discard their hand, but Narset, Parter of Veils limits each opponent to at most one replacement card, or none if they already drew that turn. Wrexial, the Risen Deep can then turn the discarded instants and sorceries into free casts.
- **Send the Bill to Their Library** (Mesmeric Orb + Bloodchief Ascension + Wrexial, the Risen Deep): A hit from Wrexial, the Risen Deep can qualify Bloodchief Ascension for a quest counter at the end step. Once it has three quest counters, each card Mesmeric Orb mills into an opponent’s graveyard can drain that player for 2 life and gain you 2.
- **Draw the Table’s New Hands** (Notion Thief + Windfall + Reliquary Tower): With Notion Thief in play, Windfall makes opponents discard their hands and gives you their replacement draws in addition to your own. Reliquary Tower lets you keep the oversized hand through cleanup.
- **Turn Life Into Cards and Spells** (Bolas's Citadel + Sensei's Divining Top): Sensei's Divining Top rearranges the top three cards to help Bolas's Citadel keep casting, or taps to draw a card that is blocking the way and puts itself on top. You can then cast Sensei's Divining Top from the top for 1 life and repeat as long as your life and library allow.

## Background

### Web research about the commander

**Build Wrexial, the Risen Deep as Dimir control with a mill-and-theft package.** The key is to put useful spells in an opponent’s graveyard *and* connect with that same opponent. You don’t need to mill their whole library to get value. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/wrexial-the-risen-deep))

1. **Main archetypes.** **Mill** is the clearest EDHREC theme: some lists mill just enough to fuel the commander, while dedicated lists aim to deck opponents or finish with a combo. **Theft/reanimator control** uses opposing graveyards as a second hand and can win without its commander. **Sea-monster control** is a more thematic build, using large threats and effects that help the commander connect. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/wrexial-the-risen-deep))
2. **Key synergies.** Windfall stocks graveyards while refilling hands; Fractured Sanity mills the whole table; Consuming Aberration keeps milling as you cast spells, and Drown in the Loch turns stocked graveyards into cheap interaction. Memory Plunder and Breach the Multiverse let you steal value without connecting in combat. Spreading Seas turns a blocker-heavy opponent’s land into an Island, while Lightning Greaves gives your six-mana commander haste and protection. Stormtide Leviathan enables islandwalk across the table and still lets your commander attack. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/wrexial-the-risen-deep))
3. **Popular combos.**
   - Mindcrank + Bloodchief Ascension: With three quest counters on the enchantment, an opponent losing life or putting a card into their graveyard starts a loop of mill, life loss, and life gain for you. It can kill the affected opponent; each opponent needs a trigger to start their own loop. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/936-1290/?utm_source=openai))
   - Mindcrank + Duskmantle Guildmage: Resolve the Guildmage’s first ability, then make an opponent lose life or put a card into their graveyard that turn. The loop mills them and drains their life; a table-wide starter can set it off for everyone. Neither loop guarantees lethal life loss if the affected library runs out first. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/936-5069/?q=mindcrank&utm_source=openai))
4. **Common cuts and mistakes.** In theft-first lists, I’d cut Traumatize before repeatable or table-wide mill: five mana to stock one graveyard is a lot when you only need one good spell. Don’t overload on expensive threats or mill-only cards at the expense of draw, interaction, and protection. Don’t mill a reanimator player without a plan, and don’t wipe all opposing graveyards right before combat. Pick your attack target by **both** landwalk and available spells. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/wrexial-the-risen-deep/mill?utm_source=openai))
5. **Mana base.** Start around **37 lands and 9–10 ramp pieces**, emphasizing two-mana rocks like Arcane Signet, Dimir Signet, and Talisman of Dominance. The commander costs **{3}{U}{B}{B}**, so prioritize reliable black sources and untapped fixing like Watery Grave and Underground River. Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth makes every opponent’s lands Swamps in addition to their other types, turning on swampwalk across the table. Rogue's Passage is a backup, not a priority: it produces colorless mana and is expensive to activate. Bojuka Bog is useful when you need it, but aim it at the graveyard you need to stop, not the one you plan to steal from. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/wrexial-the-risen-deep))
