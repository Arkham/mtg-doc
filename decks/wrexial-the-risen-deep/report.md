### How to play

Wrexial, the Risen Deep steals opponents’ graveyard spells while your artifacts build big mana. Win through evasive attacks, copied threats, or a huge Exsanguinate.

**Early game**

- Use Sol Ring and Dimir Signet to accelerate toward Wrexial, the Risen Deep.
- Deploy Rhystic Study or Sensei's Divining Top to improve your access to cards.
- Find Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth with Expedition Map to enable swampwalk and Cabal Coffers.

**Mid game**

- Equip Wrexial, the Risen Deep with Lightning Greaves for immediate attacks.
- Attack opponents holding useful graveyard spells; Dreamborn Muse and Mindcrank help stock them.
- Copy Wrexial, the Risen Deep with Sakashima the Impostor for two spell-stealing attackers.

**Late game**

- Overload Cyclonic Rift to clear blockers while preserving your threats.
- Kick Rite of Replication on Consecrated Sphinx to create an enormous draw engine.
- Fuel Exsanguinate with Cabal Coffers and Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth to drain everyone.

**How it wins**

- Wrexial, the Risen Deep threatens commander damage; Sakashima the Impostor threatens regular combat damage.
- Exsanguinate converts your huge mana production into a table-wide lethal drain.
- Bribery and Rite of Replication turn powerful creatures into your attacking finishers.

**Key cards**

- Wrexial, the Risen Deep: Turns combat hits into stolen instants and sorceries.
- Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth: Enables Wrexial’s swampwalk and supercharges Cabal Coffers.
- Lightning Greaves: Gives Wrexial haste and protection from targeted interaction.
- Tezzeret the Seeker: Untaps Mana Vault and Basalt Monolith for repeated mana bursts.
- Academy Ruins: Returns Oblivion Stone to your library for repeated board resets.
- Necropotence: Trades life for cards; Exsanguinate replenishes that life.

**Mulligan:** Keep three lands, access to blue and black, and acceleration like Dimir Signet or Sol Ring.

**Watch out for**

- Stormtide Leviathan does not prevent Wrexial, the Risen Deep from attacking, because Wrexial has islandwalk.
- Lightning Greaves and Whispersilk Cloak prevent your own Rite of Replication from targeting equipped creatures.
- Necropotence delivers cards at your end step; Reliquary Tower prevents forced discards.

### Synergies

- **Unblockable, Immediate Wrexial** (Wrexial, the Risen Deep + Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth + Lightning Greaves): Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth makes every opponent's lands Swamps, enabling Wrexial's swampwalk against anyone who controls a land. Lightning Greaves adds haste and shroud, letting Wrexial attack immediately while protected from targeted removal.
- **Two Krakens, Twice the Theft** (Wrexial, the Risen Deep + Sakashima the Impostor): Sakashima the Impostor can copy Wrexial while retaining its own name, allowing both to coexist despite the legend rule. Each has islandwalk, swampwalk, and its own combat-damage spell-theft trigger.
- **Combat and Drain Become Mill** (Wrexial, the Risen Deep + Mindcrank + Exsanguinate): Mindcrank turns Wrexial's combat damage into milling and makes Exsanguinate mill every opponent for the life they lose. This supplies more targets for future Wrexial triggers, while Exsanguinate also replenishes your life total.
- **Reusable Board Reset** (Academy Ruins + Oblivion Stone): After sacrificing Oblivion Stone to clear the board, Academy Ruins puts it back on top of your library for another use. If time and mana permit, a fate counter can preserve Wrexial through the destruction.
- **Recharge the Big Mana Rocks** (Tezzeret the Seeker + Mana Vault + Basalt Monolith): Tezzeret the Seeker's +1 untaps Mana Vault and Basalt Monolith without paying their normal untap costs. Untapping both after tapping them produces another six colorless mana, accelerating Wrexial, expensive interaction, or a large Exsanguinate.
- **Multiply the Draw Engine** (Consecrated Sphinx + Rite of Replication): A kicked Rite of Replication targeting Consecrated Sphinx creates five additional Sphinxes. With the original still present, each card an opponent draws can yield twelve cards for you.
- **Drain Life, Buy Cards** (Necropotence + Exsanguinate): Exsanguinate replenishes the life spent on Necropotence, gaining life from every opponent hit. Necropotence in turn helps find Exsanguinate and the mana sources needed to make it a substantial drain.
- **Keep Everything You Paid For** (Necropotence + Reliquary Tower): Reliquary Tower lets you retain a large Necropotence refill through cleanup rather than discard down to seven. This preserves the cards you paid life for and avoids Necropotence exiling those excess discards.
- **Reject a Stale Top Three** (Ponder + Sensei's Divining Top): Sensei's Divining Top identifies when your next three cards are unhelpful; Ponder then lets you shuffle them away and draw from a fresh library. If the cards are useful instead, their combined ordering effects help plan your upcoming draws.

### Strategy

Build Wrexial as optimized Dimir control-combo: use efficient interaction and premium draw engines to establish control, then exploit stocked opposing graveyards through Wrexial. Replace expensive theft and standalone mill with wheel disruption, compact Mindcrank finishes, and Hullbreaker Horror mana loops, retaining Exsanguinate and Blue Sun's Zenith as outlets. This pushes power within the supplied Bracket 4 rules and the 15-swap limit; reserve wins and game-ending wheel locks for turn four or later rather than pursuing an early cEDH race.

### How to play the suggested deck

This deck fills opposing graveyards, then uses Wrexial, the Risen Deep to steal their best spells. It wins through Mindcrank loops, big Exsanguinate drains, or repeated unblockable Kraken attacks.

**Early game**

- Start with Sol Ring or Dimir Signet to accelerate toward your six-mana commander.
- Use Trinket Mage to find Sol Ring, Mana Vault, or Sensei's Divining Top.
- Deploy Bloodchief Ascension early so opponents losing life can build its three quest counters.

**Mid game**

- Equip Wrexial, the Risen Deep with Lightning Greaves for immediate attacks and protection.
- Find Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth with Expedition Map to make Wrexial, the Risen Deep unblockable.
- Use Dreamborn Muse to stock opposing graveyards for Wrexial, the Risen Deep.

**Late game**

- Pair Notion Thief with Windfall to steal opponents' draws after everyone discards.
- Overload Cyclonic Rift to clear blockers and opposing defenses before your finishing turn.
- Use Tezzeret the Seeker to untap Mana Vault and Basalt Monolith for bigger Exsanguinate drains.

**How it wins**

- Combine three-counter Bloodchief Ascension with Mindcrank, then trigger opposing life loss or graveyard filling.
- Activate Duskmantle Guildmage's first ability with Mindcrank out, then mill opponents to start lethal loops.
- Finish depleted opponents with Exsanguinate or repeated attacks from Wrexial, the Risen Deep.

**Key cards**

- Wrexial, the Risen Deep: Turns combat damage into free opposing instants and sorceries.
- Mindcrank: Converts life loss into mill and enables lethal loops.
- Narset, Parter of Veils: Makes Windfall and Whispering Madness devastate opposing hands.
- Animate Dead: Recruits the best creature from any stocked graveyard.
- Academy Ruins: Recovers destroyed combo artifacts or repeatedly retrieves Oblivion Stone.

**Mulligan:** Keep two or three lands, blue and black access, and Sol Ring or Dimir Signet, ideally with Ponder.

**Watch out for**

- Hullbreaker Horror pairs with Sol Ring or Mana Vault for loops.
- Lightning Greaves prevents your Rite of Replication from targeting the equipped creature.
- Graveyard exile shuts down Wrexial, the Risen Deep's theft and disrupts Mindcrank loops.

- **Cipher-Powered Hand Suppression** (Wrexial, the Risen Deep + Whispering Madness + Narset, Parter of Veils): Encode Whispering Madness on Wrexial to turn his evasive combat hits into repeated wheels, while Narset, Parter of Veils limits each opponent to at most one card drawn that turn. The discarded cards stock future Wrexial attacks; newly discarded spells cannot become targets of a Wrexial trigger already on the stack.
- **Steal the Entire Windfall** (Notion Thief + Windfall): With Notion Thief on the battlefield, Windfall discards everyone's hands but redirects all opponents' Windfall draws to you. This creates a massive hand advantage while filling opposing graveyards for Wrexial.
- **Upkeep Mill Becomes Life Drain** (Bloodchief Ascension + Dreamborn Muse): Once Bloodchief Ascension has three quest counters, every card Dreamborn Muse mills from an opponent can make that opponent lose 2 life and you gain 2 life. This turns the deck's graveyard-filling engine into a substantial multiplayer finisher without requiring an infinite combo.
- **Clear the Citadel's Path** (Ponder + Bolas's Citadel): Ponder rearranges upcoming cards to keep Bolas's Citadel casting spells, draws away a blocking land, or shuffles away an unhelpful top three. Casting Ponder from the library costs just 1 life through the Citadel.
- **Cantrip into Disruption** (Ponder + Hullbreaker Horror): Ponder becomes a one-mana bounce trigger that replaces itself while finding your next play. Hullbreaker Horror can clear a blocker for Wrexial, the Risen Deep or remove another troublesome nonland permanent before Ponder resolves.

## Background

### Web research about the commander

#### 1. Main strategies/archetypes

Current EDHREC data and published decklists point to three overlapping approaches—not one settled “best” build: ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/wrexial-the-risen-deep))

- **Mill into spell theft/control:** Fill opposing graveyards, connect with **Wrexial, the Risen Deep**, and cast their best instants/sorceries. Mill can supply resources rather than be the primary win condition. ([tappedout.net](https://tappedout.net/mtg-decks/12-11-19-wrexial-the-risen-deep/))
- **Dedicated mill/combo:** Use repeatable mill and library-halving effects, with **Duskmantle Guildmage**, **Bruvac the Grandiloquent**, or **Fraying Sanity** as finishers. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/wrexial-the-risen-deep/mill))
- **Graveyard theft/reanimator, sometimes with discard:** Exploit milled creatures as well as spells. **Sea-monster/landwalk** builds are another recognizable branch; **Gyruda, Doom of Depths** companion builds are a smaller niche. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/wrexial-the-risen-deep))

#### 2. Key synergy cards and engines

| Role | Specific cards and purpose |
|---|---|
| **Stock graveyards** | **Ruin Crab**, **Fractured Sanity**, **Maddening Cacophony**, **Nemesis of Reason**, **Consuming Aberration**. The last two provide repeatable mill rather than only a one-shot effect. |
| **Exploit those graveyards** | **Memory Plunder**, **Diluvian Primordial** for spell theft; **Extract from Darkness**, **Sepulchral Primordial**, **Geth, Lord of the Vault** for creature theft/reanimation. |
| **Graveyard payoffs/control** | **Drown in the Loch**, **Syr Konrad, the Grim**, **Lazav, Dimir Mastermind**, **Phenax, God of Deception**. |

These packages recur in EDHREC recommendations and actual Wrexial lists. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/wrexial-the-risen-deep/mill))

- **Make Wrexial connect:** **Spreading Seas**, **Sea's Claim**, **Quicksilver Fountain**, **Harbinger of the Seas**, and **Blanket of Night** enable landwalk. **Lightning Greaves** and **Swiftfoot Boots** help a six-mana commander survive and attack promptly. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/wrexial-the-risen-deep))
- **Sea-monster package:** **Whelming Wave** leaves Wrexial behind; **Stormtide Leviathan**, **Serpent of Yawning Depths**, and **Spawning Kraken** support evasive sea-monster combat. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/wrexial-the-risen-deep))

#### 3. Popular combos—exact outputs

These appear on Wrexial’s EDHREC combo page; **none requires Wrexial himself**. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/combos/wrexial-the-risen-deep))

- **Duskmantle Guildmage + Mindcrank:** Activate the Guildmage’s first ability for **{1}{U}{B}**, then cause an opponent to lose life or put a card into their graveyard. Mill causes life loss, which causes more mill. **Result: a lethal life-loss/mill loop**, provided the opponent has enough cards to sustain it; seed each opponent to eliminate the table. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/936-5069/))
- **Bruvac the Grandiloquent + Maddening Cacophony:** Cast **Maddening Cacophony** kicked, costing **{4}{U}{U}** total. Bruvac doubles the rounded-up half-library mill. **Result: every opponent mills their entire library**, absent replacement effects or other interference—not literally an infinite loop. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/95-5080/))
- **Fraying Sanity + Maddening Cacophony:** A kicked Cacophony mills half of every opponent’s library; at the end step, **Fraying Sanity** mills the enchanted opponent’s remainder. **Result: one opponent’s library emptied**, with the others approximately halved. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/combos/mono-blue/464-5080))
- **Fraying Sanity + Traumatize:** **Result: the enchanted opponent mills their whole library if its starting size is even; ordinarily one card remains if odd**, unless another card entered their graveyard that turn. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/combos/mono-blue/464-781))

#### 4. Common mistakes / underperformers

*These are interaction-based cautions and deckbuilding judgments, not a measured community “worst cards” ranking.*

- **The Mindskinner is a major nonbo:** It prevents your creatures’ damage to opponents, so Wrexial does **not** deal combat damage and does **not** trigger spell theft. It belongs in a committed mill plan, not automatically in a Wrexial-value deck. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/648-6003/))
- **Too much small, single-opponent mill/discard:** **Glimpse the Unthinkable**, standalone **Traumatize**, and **Mind Rot** need sufficient payoffs; otherwise they spend cards without controlling the board. EDHREC’s discard article explicitly flags Mind Rot’s poor multiplayer scaling. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/articles/singleton-shmingleton-build-a-theft-deck-with-mind-rot))
- **Overloading expensive sea monsters:** My assessment: **Fleet Swallower** and **Stormtide Leviathan** are defensible themed finishers, but too many such cards compete with casting—and recasting—Wrexial. Keep early ramp and interaction intact. ([mtggoldfish.com](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/7182936))

#### 5. Mana base advice specific to Wrexial

- **Start around 36–38 lands**, then tune to curve and draw. Wrexial costs **{3}{U}{U}{B}**: prioritize reliable double blue. Use **Sol Ring**, **Arcane Signet**, **Dimir Signet**, and **Talisman of Dominance** to reach six mana earlier. This is a starting recommendation, consistent with sampled lists—not an optimized universal count. ([gatherer.wizards.com](https://gatherer.wizards.com/WWK/en-us/120/wrexial-the-risen-deep))
- **Fixing:** **Command Tower**, **Watery Grave**, **Morphic Pool**, **Underground River**, **Drowned Catacomb**, **Shipwreck Marsh**, and **Polluted Delta**; budget options include **Choked Estuary** and **Sunken Hollow** with adequate basics. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/wrexial-the-risen-deep/upgraded/expensive/average-deck))
- **Exceptional commander synergy:** **Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth** makes opponents’ lands Swamps, enabling Wrexial’s swampwalk while improving your black mana. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/209-233-714/))
- **Build around your own land conversion:** **Harbinger of the Seas** also turns *your* nonbasic lands into Islands. Retain basic **Swamp** cards and artifact black sources; otherwise your evasion enabler can prevent you from casting Wrexial. ([gatherer.wizards.com](https://gatherer.wizards.com/MH3/en-us/63/harbinger-of-the-seas))
