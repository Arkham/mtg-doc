### How to play

Get Wrexial, the Risen Deep through unblocked and cast the best spells from opponents’ graveyards. Close with commander damage, stolen threats, or a big Exsanguinate.

**Early game**

- Play Dimir Signet early so your colorless mana can help cast Wrexial, the Risen Deep.
- Use Expedition Map to find whichever half of your big mana pair is missing.
- Find Expedition Map or Mana Vault with Trinket Mage, depending on what you need.

**Mid game**

- Pair Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth with Cabal Coffers for mana and unblockable commander attacks.
- Attack with Wrexial, the Risen Deep where you can connect and steal the best spell.
- Copy Wrexial, the Risen Deep with Sakashima the Impostor to steal spells with both.

**Late game**

- Copy Thran Dynamo with Sculpting Steel, then untap both with Tezzeret the Seeker.
- Recycle Expedition Map with Academy Ruins to find another key land.
- Overload Cyclonic Rift before attacking with the threats you stole through Blatant Thievery.

**How it wins**

- Land five hits with Wrexial, the Risen Deep to eliminate a player through commander damage.
- Pour mana from Cabal Coffers into Exsanguinate to drain the whole table.
- Use Bribery to steal the creature most likely to win through combat.

**Key cards**

- Wrexial, the Risen Deep: Turns unblocked hits into free spells from opposing graveyards.
- Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth: Turns opposing lands into swamps so your commander slips through.
- Cabal Coffers: Produces the big mana your finishers need.
- Lightning Greaves: Lets your commander attack immediately and dodge targeted removal.
- Exsanguinate: Converts excess mana into life loss for every opponent.

**Mulligan:** Keep two or three lands with blue access and early acceleration like Dimir Signet or Sol Ring.

**Watch out for**

- Graveyard exile can remove the spell Wrexial, the Risen Deep targets before you cast it.
- Lightning Greaves also stops your own Rite of Replication from targeting the equipped creature.
- Without Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth, Cabal Coffers may produce too little mana to help.

### Strategy

Play Dimir control: use cheap interaction and tutors to set up Mindcrank with either Duskmantle Guildmage or Bloodchief Ascension, so you can win without resolving your six-mana commander. Windfall stocks opposing graveyards for Wrexial, the Risen Deep, while Lightning Greaves lets him attack before opponents untap. Free countermagic, fast mana, and compact combos put the deck firmly in Optimized Bracket 4, with a game plan that expects at least four turns rather than racing for a pre-turn-four win.

### How to play the suggested deck

Wrexial, the Risen Deep turns discarded and milled spells into free casts. Finish with a Mindcrank loop or pour mana from Cabal Coffers into Exsanguinate.

**Early game**

- Use Sol Ring to deploy Dimir Signet and get both commander colors online.
- Use Expedition Map to find whichever half of Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth and Cabal Coffers you're missing.
- Land Rhystic Study early so opposing spells can refill your hand while you ramp.

**Mid game**

- Equip Lightning Greaves to Wrexial, the Risen Deep for an immediate swing.
- Attack whoever has the best spell in their graveyard that Wrexial, the Risen Deep can reach.
- Flash in Notion Thief before Windfall to refill your hand and stock opposing graveyards.

**Late game**

- Use Tezzeret the Seeker to fetch Mindcrank or untap Thran Dynamo before casting Exsanguinate.
- Copy Wrexial, the Risen Deep with Sakashima the Impostor to steal two spells per combat.

**How it wins**

- With Mindcrank out, activate Duskmantle Guildmage’s life-loss ability, then make an opponent mill or lose life.
- Pair Mindcrank with a fully charged Bloodchief Ascension, then make each opponent mill or lose life.
- Fuel a lethal Exsanguinate with Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth and Cabal Coffers.

**Key cards**

- Wrexial, the Risen Deep: Turns combat damage into free spells from the defending player's graveyard.
- Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth: Enables swampwalk and makes every land count for Cabal Coffers.
- Mindcrank: Turns life loss into milling for both graveyard loops.
- Windfall: Stocks opposing graveyards and refills your hand with Notion Thief.

**Mulligan:** Keep lands covering both colors, early ramp like Dimir Signet, and draw or cheap interaction.

**Watch out for**

- Graveyard exile can remove the spell you target with Wrexial, the Risen Deep.
- Lightning Greaves prevents your own Sakashima the Impostor from targeting nothing because copying doesn't target.

## Background

### Web research about the commander

**Wrexial, the Risen Deep is best built as a theft-control commander with a mill package.** Mill is EDHREC’s largest tagged archetype, but you don’t have to empty a library to get value: you just need a good instant or sorcery in the graveyard of the opponent you hit. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/wrexial-the-risen-deep))

1. **Main builds.** **Mill** either stocks opposing graveyards or commits to decking the table. **Theft/control** uses discard, removal, and selective milling to set up profitable attacks. **Reanimator** makes use of the creatures you mill, while **sea-creature builds** lean into big threats and combat. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/wrexial-the-risen-deep))
2. **Key engines.** Windfall refills your hand and stocks graveyards; Drown in the Loch turns those stocked yards into cheap interaction. Tasha, the Witch Queen gives you another way to cast opposing spells, and Sepulchral Primordial turns milled creatures into your board. Lightning Greaves gives your six-mana commander a chance to connect before opponents untap. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/wrexial-the-risen-deep))
3. **Common combo finishes.**
   - **Duskmantle Guildmage + Mindcrank:** Activate the Guildmage’s first ability, then make an opponent lose life or put a card into their graveyard. Mill and life loss feed each other, usually killing that opponent; each opponent needs a spark. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/combos/wrexial-the-risen-deep))
   - **Bruvac the Grandiloquent + kicked Maddening Cacophony:** Mills every opponent’s entire library, barring replacement effects. They lose when they next try to draw, not when the spell resolves. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/combos/wrexial-the-risen-deep))
4. **What I’d cut first.** In theft/control, skip small one-shot mill like Mind Sculpt: it costs a card without affecting the board or guaranteeing a useful spell. Avoid blanket graveyard hate like Rest in Peace, which shuts off your own engine. And don’t overload on expensive sea creatures unless that’s the plan—the commander already competes for your six-mana turn. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/wrexial-the-risen-deep/theft?utm_source=openai))
5. **Mana base.** Start around **37 lands** with cheap ramp such as Arcane Signet, Talisman of Dominance, and Dimir Signet. Prioritize untapped colored sources like Underground River and Watery Grave: you need two blue and one black to cast the commander, plus enough early blue for interaction. Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth is especially valuable because it gives opponents’ lands the Swamp type, turning on swampwalk against anyone who controls a land. Keep colorless utility lands and slow taplands to a minimum. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/wrexial-the-risen-deep))
