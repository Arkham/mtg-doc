### How to play

This deck turns Prossh, Skyraider of Kher’s Kobolds into cards, mana, and sacrifice pressure. Win with token damage from Purphoros, God of the Forge, sacrifice loops, or a boosted swarm.

**Early game**

- Use Cultivate or Kodama's Reach to prepare for Prossh, Skyraider of Kher.
- Deploy Awakening Zone for recurring sacrifice fodder that can also provide mana.
- Find Forest duals with Skyshroud Claim, paying life if you need them untapped.

**Mid game**

- Cast Prossh, Skyraider of Kher for Kobolds, then equip Skullclamp to draw cards.
- Sacrifice Kobolds to Prossh, Skyraider of Kher while Grave Pact clears opposing creatures.
- Use Xenagos, the Reveler to turn your token count into a huge mana burst.

**Late game**

- Pair Tooth and Claw with Parallel Lives or Primal Vigor, starting with two creatures.
- Steal creatures with Insurrection, attack, then sacrifice survivors to Goblin Bombardment.

**How it wins**

- Loop Tooth and Claw with Parallel Lives to win through Purphoros, God of the Forge.
- Cast Triumph of the Hordes when your swarm can deliver lethal poison.
- Give Prossh, Skyraider of Kher trample with Kessig Wolf Run, sacrificing creatures for lethal commander damage.

**Key cards**

- Skullclamp: Turns each unboosted Kobold into two cards.
- Purphoros, God of the Forge: Damages every opponent whenever your other creatures enter.
- Tooth and Claw: Loops token creation with either doubler and two starting creatures.
- Perilous Forays: Converts spare tokens into lands with basic land types.
- Ophiomancer: Replaces sacrificed Snakes at each upkeep.

**Mulligan:** Keep three lands with green access, Cultivate or Sakura-Tribe Elder, and a payoff like Skullclamp.

**Watch out for**

- Primal Vigor doubles opponents’ tokens and creature counters too.
- Fecundity lets opponents draw when their creatures die, including from Grave Pact.
- Remove Lightning Greaves before targeting Prossh, Skyraider of Kher with Kessig Wolf Run.

### Synergies

- **Clamp the Kobolds** (Prossh, Skyraider of Kher + Skullclamp): Prossh, Skyraider of Kher supplies 0/1 tokens that die immediately when equipped with Skullclamp, turning each equip payment into two cards. A normal six-mana commander cast provides enough tokens to draw twelve cards over successive equips.
- **One-Sided Creature Purge** (Prossh, Skyraider of Kher + Grave Pact): Sacrificing tokens to Prossh, Skyraider of Kher triggers Grave Pact for each death, forcing every opponent to sacrifice a creature. This converts expendable tokens into repeatable removal while increasing your commander's power.
- **Doubled Entry Burn** (Prossh, Skyraider of Kher + Purphoros, God of the Forge + Parallel Lives): With Parallel Lives and Purphoros, God of the Forge in play, casting Prossh, Skyraider of Kher for six mana creates twelve tokens that deal 24 damage to each opponent through entry triggers. Your commander entering adds another 2 damage to each opponent.
- **A Horde That Makes Mana** (Prossh, Skyraider of Kher + Xenagos, the Reveler): A six-mana cast of Prossh, Skyraider of Kher adds seven creatures to the battlefield, increasing the mana produced by the +1 ability of Xenagos, the Reveler by seven. Unlike sacrifice-based ramp, this leaves the entire army available for combat or later sacrifice effects.

### Strategy

Build around Prossh, Skyraider of Kher as a token engine, converting Kobolds into mana, cards, and lethal sacrifice triggers. Food Chain and the altar engines provide repeatable commander casts, while Zulaport Cutthroat, Purphoros, God of the Forge, and Goblin Bombardment turn those loops into wins. Efficient tutors, stronger draw, and protection make this an optimized Bracket 4 combo-aristocrats deck intended for turn-four-or-later wins rather than a dedicated early-turn turbo strategy.

### How to play the suggested deck

This deck turns Prossh, Skyraider of Kher’s Kobolds into mana, cards, and sacrifice triggers. Win through Food Chain loops, Zulaport Cutthroat drains, or a token swarm boosted by Triumph of the Hordes.

**Early game**

- Use Birds of Paradise and Cultivate to assemble Prossh, Skyraider of Kher’s colors.
- Set up Skullclamp before casting Prossh, Skyraider of Kher for immediate card draw.

**Mid game**

- Equip Kobolds with Skullclamp to draw two cards per token.
- Sacrifice Kobolds to Viscera Seer with Grave Pact active to clear opposing creatures.
- Use Xenagos, the Reveler after making Kobolds to generate a huge mana burst.

**Late game**

- Find Food Chain with Demonic Tutor when Prossh, Skyraider of Kher can start looping.
- Cast Triumph of the Hordes when your token swarm can connect with opponents.

**How it wins**

- Repeated Prossh, Skyraider of Kher casts with Food Chain trigger Purphoros, God of the Forge.
- Loop Reassembling Skeleton, Pitiless Plunderer, and Ashnod's Altar to drain opponents through Zulaport Cutthroat.
- Attack with seven creatures to activate Beastmaster Ascension and overwhelm blockers.

**Key cards**

- Prossh, Skyraider of Kher: Creates sacrifice fodder even if opponents counter the creature spell.
- Food Chain: Exiles Kobolds and your commander to fund repeated commander casts.
- Skullclamp: Turns each zero-toughness Kobold into two cards.
- Pitiless Plunderer: Replaces dying creatures with Treasures and enables sacrifice loops.
- Purphoros, God of the Forge: Turns every entering Kobold into damage against each opponent.

**Mulligan:** Keep lands covering your colors, early acceleration like Sol Ring, and Skullclamp or Sylvan Library for cards.

**Watch out for**

- Food Chain exiles creatures, so it cannot trigger Zulaport Cutthroat or Grave Pact.
- Fecundity draws cards for opponents too, while Primal Vigor doubles their tokens.
- Move Lightning Greaves away before targeting your commander with Kessig Wolf Run.

### Synergies the swaps add

- **Every-Upkeep Drain** (Ophiomancer + Viscera Seer + Zulaport Cutthroat): Sacrifice each Snake from Ophiomancer to Viscera Seer before the next upkeep, leaving you eligible to create another Snake. With Zulaport Cutthroat, each sacrifice also drains every opponent and gains you life, producing cards-selection and life swings throughout the turn cycle.
- **Turn Tokens into a New Hand** (Prossh, Skyraider of Kher + Ashnod's Altar + Fecundity): Sacrificing tokens from Prossh, Skyraider of Kher to Ashnod's Altar produces two colorless mana and an optional draw from Fecundity per token. Six tokens can become twelve colorless mana and six cards, providing both resources to continue your turn, though Fecundity also rewards opponents when their creatures die.

## Background

### Web research about the commander

#### 1. Main strategies

Current EDHREC data puts **aristocrats, tokens, and combo** at the center of Prossh, Skyraider of Kher builds. These overlap heavily: make Kobolds, convert them into resources, then drain or combo out. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/prossh-skyraider-of-kher))

- **Aristocrats/token burn:** sacrifice creatures for death triggers, or damage everyone when tokens enter.
- **Go-wide combat:** turn the token army into attackers with Beastmaster Ascension.
- **Commander damage:** sacrifice creatures to grow Prossh, Skyraider of Kher; this is an established alternative win condition.
- **Dedicated combo:** repeatedly cast the commander using Food Chain or sacrifice-based mana engines. ([blog.cardkingdom.com](https://blog.cardkingdom.com/commander-on-a-budget-prossh/))

#### 2. Key synergy cards and engines

- **Draw:** Skullclamp converts each unbuffed Kobold into two cards; Fecundity and Korvold, Fae-Cursed King support sacrifice-driven draw engines.
- **Damage/drain:** Impact Tremors, Purphoros, God of the Forge, Zulaport Cutthroat, Blood Artist, Mayhem Devil, and Mirkwood Bats reward the deck’s normal token-and-sacrifice play.
- **Resources and outlets:** Goblin Bombardment turns bodies into damage; Ashnod's Altar and Phyrexian Altar turn them into mana; Pitiless Plunderer supplies Treasures.
- **Token multiplication:** Chatterfang, Squirrel General and Parallel Lives increase both value and combo potential. These are established inclusions, not merely speculative upgrades. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/prossh-skyraider-of-kher))

#### 3. Popular combos

For all three loops below, Prossh, Skyraider of Kher must be your commander, and you need enough starting mana to cast it, including any commander tax.

| Exact pieces | What they produce |
|---|---|
| Prossh, Skyraider of Kher + Food Chain | Repeatedly exile the commander and enough Kobolds, then recast. Produces arbitrarily many tokens, creature entries, commander casts, and **mana usable only to cast creature spells**. Sacrificing surplus tokens to the commander also produces arbitrarily many death/sacrifice triggers and arbitrarily large power. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/1864-3519/)) |
| Prossh, Skyraider of Kher + Ashnod's Altar + Phyrexian Altar | Sacrifice the commander and tokens between the two altars to fund increasingly expensive recasts. Produces arbitrarily much unrestricted colored/colorless mana, tokens, casts, and death/sacrifice triggers. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/1864-2034-4050/)) |
| Prossh, Skyraider of Kher + Phyrexian Altar + Chatterfang, Squirrel General | Extra Squirrels make each recast profitable. Produces arbitrarily much colored mana, tokens, casts, and death/sacrifice triggers. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/1864-3000-4050/)) |

**Winning outlet:** Impact Tremors or Purphoros, God of the Forge converts repeated creature entries into lethal damage. With the first loop, have a noncreature payoff already deployed or retain ordinary mana to cast it. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/prossh-skyraider-of-kher))

#### 4. Common mistakes / underperformers

These are **build-dependent cautions**, not universal cut lists:

- **Using nontoken-only rewards in a token-heavy build:** Grim Haruspex does not draw cards from dying Kobolds. This is a poor fit unless the deck also sacrifices substantial numbers of nontoken creatures. ([media.wizards.com](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/SOS_Release_Notes_FwhcBWdFIE/EN_MTGSOS_ReleaseNotes_20260410.pdf?utm_source=openai))
- **Expecting reanimation or blinking to make Kobolds:** the token ability triggers on casting, not entering. Conversely, countering the commander does not automatically counter its token trigger. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/card/1864/))
- **Relying on the commander to sacrifice itself:** it sacrifices only *another* creature. Include an independent outlet when recasting is part of the plan. ([cloudflare.edhrec.com](https://cloudflare.edhrec.com/articles/non-basically-speaking-prossh-skyraider-of-kher))
- **Treating exile as death:** Food Chain does not itself trigger Blood Artist; sacrifice surplus tokens separately. Also avoid mana-intensive sacrifice outlets when free outlets can perform the job. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/1864-3519/))

#### 5. Mana base advice

- **My starting recommendation:** approximately **36–38 lands plus 10–12 ramp pieces** for ordinary casual builds; tune downward only with a lower curve and abundant cheap acceleration. Six mana—and later commander tax—makes missed land drops costly. For context, one current EDHREC average runs 35 lands alongside substantial ramp. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/prossh-skyraider-of-kher/core/average-deck?f=in%3DFood+Chain))
- **Prioritize early green while reliably finding black and red:** Birds of Paradise, Ignoble Hierarch, Nature's Lore, Three Visits, and Farseek are established options. Command Tower, Overgrown Tomb, Stomping Ground, and Blood Crypt support the three-color requirement. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/prossh-skyraider-of-kher/core/average-deck?f=in%3DFood+Chain))
- **Keep colorless utility lands selective:** High Market sacrifices the commander for a recast; Kessig Wolf Run supplies trample for commander-damage kills; Kher Keep supplies emergency fodder. Gaea's Cradle is especially powerful after a token burst. Don’t let these crowd out colored sources. ([cloudflare.edhrec.com](https://cloudflare.edhrec.com/articles/non-basically-speaking-prossh-skyraider-of-kher))
