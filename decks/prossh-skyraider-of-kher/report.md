### How to play

Prossh, Skyraider of Kher brings Kobolds even if countered, and commander tax makes later batches bigger. Turn that swarm into direct damage with Purphoros, God of the Forge or lethal attacks with Triumph of the Hordes.

**Early game**

- Use Sakura-Tribe Elder or Cultivate to find missing colors for Prossh, Skyraider of Kher.
- Set up Skullclamp early so your first Kobolds can become cards right away.

**Mid game**

- Equip Skullclamp to an unbuffed Kobold to draw two cards for one mana.
- Sacrifice your Ophiomancer Snake to Goblin Bombardment before each upkeep to get another.
- After feeding your swarm to Goblin Bombardment, cast Fresh Meat that turn to rebuild.

**Late game**

- With Parallel Lives and Purphoros, God of the Forge, six-mana Prossh, Skyraider of Kher deals 24.
- Use Xenagos, the Reveler to turn your swarm into mana for Tempt with Vengeance.

**How it wins**

- Loop two creatures through Tooth and Claw with Parallel Lives or Primal Vigor.
- Use Purphoros, God of the Forge to turn that loop into lethal damage to everyone else.
- Send a wide swarm through blockers with Triumph of the Hordes for lethal poison.

**Key cards**

- Prossh, Skyraider of Kher: Makes sacrifice fodder on cast and turns it into flying power.
- Skullclamp: Turns each unbuffed Kobold into two cards.
- Tooth and Claw: Loops creature entries and deaths with a token doubler.
- Purphoros, God of the Forge: Turns every creature entry into damage to each opponent.
- Goblin Bombardment: Turns spare tokens into removal or reach.

**Mulligan:** Keep three lands and early ramp like Cultivate, with a path to all three colors for Prossh, Skyraider of Kher.

**Watch out for**

- Primal Vigor doubles opponents’ tokens too, especially if they accept Tempt with Vengeance.
- Save mana for Fresh Meat when a sweeper threatens your swarm.
- The Tooth and Claw loop does not win by itself without a payoff.

- **Twelve Tokens, Twenty-Four Damage** (Prossh, Skyraider of Kher + Parallel Lives + Purphoros, God of the Forge): On a six-mana cast of Prossh, Skyraider of Kher, Parallel Lives turns six Kobolds into twelve, and Purphoros, God of the Forge deals 24 damage to each opponent from the tokens alone. The token trigger can resolve even if the commander spell is countered.
- **One Mana, Two Cards** (Prossh, Skyraider of Kher + Skullclamp): Skullclamp drops each unbuffed 0/1 Kobold from Prossh, Skyraider of Kher to zero toughness, drawing two cards per equip without needing a separate sacrifice outlet.
- **Cash In, Then Rebuild** (Prossh, Skyraider of Kher + Goblin Bombardment + Fresh Meat): Feed the Kobolds from Prossh, Skyraider of Kher to Goblin Bombardment for damage, then cast Fresh Meat that same turn to replace each fallen Kobold with a 3/3 Beast. In response to a board wipe, bank the damage first and wait until the wipe resolves to rebuild.
- **A Shot Every Upkeep** (Ophiomancer + Goblin Bombardment + Goblin Sharpshooter): Sacrifice the Snake from Ophiomancer to Goblin Bombardment before the next upkeep so you can get another one, including on opponents' turns. Tap Goblin Sharpshooter before sacrificing the Snake so its death untaps the Sharpshooter for another shot.
- **Turn the Swarm into Mana** (Prossh, Skyraider of Kher + Xenagos, the Reveler + Tempt with Vengeance): With Prossh, Skyraider of Kher and its first six Kobolds in play, Xenagos, the Reveler can make at least seven mana without sacrificing anything. Spend it on Tempt with Vengeance for a second wave of hasty attackers that also increases the planeswalker's mana output next turn.

### Strategy

Build toward a turn-four-or-later win with Food Chain and Prossh, Skyraider of Kher, then turn repeated token creation into lethal damage with Agate Instigator or Purphoros, God of the Forge. When exile-based combo is shut off, use the altar package to recast your commander and win through sacrifice or death triggers. Cheap fixing, efficient tutors, and repeatable draw make this a combo-forward Bracket 4 deck that can reload after disruption rather than relying on a single all-in attempt.

### How to play the suggested deck

Prossh, Skyraider of Kher makes Kobolds you can trade for cards, mana, or damage. Win by looping creature casts, burning opponents with token arrivals, or attacking with a swarm.

**Early game**

- Lead with Birds of Paradise or Ignoble Hierarch to fix your commander’s colors.
- Play Ophiomancer early to supply blockers and fodder for Goblin Bombardment.

**Mid game**

- Equip Skullclamp to a Kobold to draw two cards immediately.
- Land Grave Pact before sacrificing Kobolds to thin opposing boards.
- Sacrifice a spare token to Diabolic Intent to find Food Chain or a payoff.

**Late game**

- Set up Purphoros, God of the Forge before casting Prossh, Skyraider of Kher.
- Use Goblin Bombardment in response to a wipe, then rebuild with Fresh Meat.

**How it wins**

- Exile Prossh, Skyraider of Kher and Kobolds to Food Chain, then recast from command repeatedly.
- Turn repeated token arrivals into lethal damage with Purphoros, God of the Forge.
- Use Chatterfang, Squirrel General to widen your swarm, then attack with Triumph of the Hordes.

**Key cards**

- Prossh, Skyraider of Kher: Makes more Kobolds as commander tax rises.
- Food Chain: Turns your commander and Kobolds into repeatable creature mana.
- Skullclamp: Turns each spare Kobold into two cards.
- Purphoros, God of the Forge: Turns each token arrival into damage to every opponent.

**Mulligan:** Keep three lands with green access, early ramp like Birds of Paradise, and a payoff like Skullclamp.

**Watch out for**

- Countering Prossh, Skyraider of Kher doesn’t stop the separate Kobold trigger.
- Food Chain mana can cast creatures but can’t pay to equip Skullclamp.
- Primal Vigor doubles opponents’ tokens too, especially the ones from Tempt with Vengeance.

## Background

### Web research about the commander

#### Prossh, Skyraider of Kher — Commander deckbuilding guide

1. **Main builds:** **Aristocrats** turns Kobolds into cards, mana, and life drain. **Go-wide tokens** uses token multipliers and team-wide buffs or damage when creatures enter. **Combo** prioritizes Food Chain and altar loops. **Voltron** is a secondary plan: sacrifice the tokens to pump your flying commander for a commander-damage kill. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/prossh-skyraider-of-kher/core/aristocrats/average-deck))

2. **Key engines:** Skullclamp turns each unbuffed Kobold into two cards; Korvold, Fae-Cursed King draws on sacrifices. Goblin Bombardment converts bodies into targeted damage, Pitiless Plunderer turns deaths into Treasure, and Zulaport Cutthroat drains the table. Chatterfang, Squirrel General adds bodies, Mazirek, Kraul Death Priest grows the team, and Purphoros, God of the Forge turns token entries into damage to every opponent. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/prossh-skyraider-of-kher/core/aristocrats/average-deck))

3. **Popular combos** (each uses Prossh, Skyraider of Kher as commander): ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/card/1864/))
   - **Food Chain:** Exile the commander and enough Kobolds to pay for the next cast. Repeat for arbitrarily many tokens, casts, and creature-only mana. Purphoros, God of the Forge turns the entries into lethal damage. Exiling creatures doesn’t itself trigger deaths. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/card/1864/))
   - **Phyrexian Altar + Ashnod's Altar:** Sacrifice bodies for the colored and colorless mana needed to recast the commander. Produces arbitrarily many tokens, casts, death triggers, and unrestricted mana. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/card/1864/))
   - **Phyrexian Altar + Chatterfang, Squirrel General** (or **Parallel Lives**): The extra tokens pay for each recast with bodies left over. Produces arbitrarily many tokens, casts, death triggers, and colored mana. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/card/1864/))

4. **Common traps:** Blink and reanimation don’t make Kobolds—the ability triggers on cast, not on entry. Cost reducers can help you cast the commander sooner, but fewer mana actually spent means fewer Kobolds. In a dedicated Food Chain build, don’t count on casting Impact Tremors or Goblin Bombardment off the loop’s mana; have them in play first, or use a creature payoff. In slower builds, trim redundant expensive token multipliers before cutting early ramp or draw. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/card/1864/))

5. **Mana base:** Start at **36–37 lands** for a casual or midrange build. Prioritize untapped green for Birds of Paradise and Nature's Lore, then make sure you can reliably produce black and red by the time you have six mana. Command Tower fixes all three colors; Overgrown Tomb and Stomping Ground are fetchable with Nature's Lore or Three Visits. Kher Keep supplies fodder, but treat it as a flex slot: it taps for colorless and shouldn’t displace a needed colored source. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/prossh-skyraider-of-kher/core/aristocrats/average-deck))
