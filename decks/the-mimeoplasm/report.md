### How to play

The Mimeoplasm turns creatures from any graveyard into oversized threats. Win by attacking with your commander or sacrificing a huge creature to Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord.

**Early game**

- Fetch Overgrown Tomb with Farseek to fix your black and green mana.
- Use Grisly Salvage to find a land while stocking your graveyard.
- Use Entomb to put Wonder in your graveyard if you control an Island.

**Mid game**

- Use Buried Alive to find a creature worth copying and another with high power.
- Check opposing graveyards for better options before casting The Mimeoplasm.
- Recover lands sacrificed to Sylvan Safekeeper with Life from the Loam.

**Late game**

- Cast Living Death when your graveyard offers better creatures than your opponents’ graveyards.
- Sacrifice an oversized The Mimeoplasm to Momentous Fall in response to removal.

**How it wins**

- Copy Mindleech Mass with The Mimeoplasm and trample through blockers for commander damage.
- Copy Skithiryx, the Blight Dragon with The Mimeoplasm to threaten lethal poison.
- Feed a huge Lord of Extinction to Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord to drain every opponent.

**Key cards**

- The Mimeoplasm: Copies one creature and adds counters based on another’s power.
- Buried Alive: Loads your graveyard with three creatures of your choice.
- Lord of Extinction: Converts every graveyard’s size into power.
- Sylvan Safekeeper: Trades lands to shield your commander from targeted removal.
- Life from the Loam: Restocks sacrificed lands and fills your graveyard through dredge.

**Mulligan:** Keep three lands with green and black mana, plus Grisly Salvage or Buried Alive.

**Watch out for**

- Graveyard exile can leave The Mimeoplasm without two creatures to use.
- Creatures exiled by The Mimeoplasm won’t come back with Living Death.
- Equip Lightning Greaves after using Vorel of the Hull Clade to double your commander’s counters.

### Strategy

Use cheap ramp, looting, and self-mill to stock the graveyard, then turn that setup into pressure with The Mimeoplasm or bring back threats without paying commander tax. Survival of the Fittest and Gifts Ungiven find the creatures you need, while Cyclonic Rift buys time or clears the way for a decisive attack. This is upper-end Bracket 3: all three Game Changer slots are used, with no mass land denial, extra turns, or two-card infinite combos, and the deck aims to close from turn six onward rather than rush an early knockout.

### How to play the suggested deck

Fill graveyards, then use The Mimeoplasm to turn two creatures into one oversized attacker. Win through combat or sacrifice a huge creature to Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord to drain everyone.

**Early game**

- Use Nature's Lore to fetch Breeding Pool, which also enables flying from Wonder.
- Pitch Titanoth Rex with Careful Study to set up a trampling commander.
- Use Skull Prophet for mana early, then mill yourself once your colors are set.

**Mid game**

- Use Buried Alive to bin Titanoth Rex, Yargle and Multani, and Wonder.
- Make The Mimeoplasm copy Titanoth Rex using Yargle and Multani for eighteen extra counters.
- Sacrifice Sakura-Tribe Elder to Victimize to bring back Sheoldred, Whispering One and Eternal Witness.

**Late game**

- Use Life from the Loam to recover lands sacrificed to Sylvan Safekeeper.
- Let Mesmeric Orb grow Lord of Extinction before sacrificing it to Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord.
- Equip Lightning Greaves before attacking with a freshly cast The Mimeoplasm.

**How it wins**

- Two unblocked hits from The Mimeoplasm copying Titanoth Rex with eighteen extra counters deal lethal commander damage.
- Sacrifice a giant Lord of Extinction to Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord to drain every opponent.

**Key cards**

- The Mimeoplasm: Builds a giant attacker from creatures in any graveyard.
- Buried Alive: Sets up both commander targets and leaves Wonder behind.
- Mesmeric Orb: Fills every graveyard and grows Lord of Extinction.
- Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord: Turns one huge creature into life loss for everyone else.

**Mulligan:** Keep three lands, early acceleration like Nature's Lore, and graveyard setup like Careful Study.

**Watch out for**

- Graveyard exile can strand The Mimeoplasm, so avoid committing every threat at once.
- Check opponents' graveyards before casting Living Death, especially after Mesmeric Orb has been milling everyone.
- Use Vorel of the Hull Clade before equipping Lightning Greaves, since shroud blocks Vorel's ability.

## Background

### Web research about the commander

#### 1. Main archetypes

- **Self-mill / reanimator:** Fill your graveyard, then bring back threats cheaply. The Mimeoplasm is a payoff, not your only way to use the graveyard.
- **Big-power / counters:** Pair a high-power creature with a copy target that turns those counters into damage. That can mean direct damage with Walking Ballista or a one-hit poison kill with Skithiryx, the Blight Dragon if it connects with at least 10 power.
- **Graveyard combo:** Use creature tutors to assemble an activated-ability kill around Necrotic Ooze. Ooze typal is also a theme, but it’s a different build from the dedicated combo deck. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/the-mimeoplasm))

#### 2. Key cards and engines

| Job | Cards |
|---|---|
| Fill the graveyard | Entomb finds one exact piece; Buried Alive finds three. Stitcher's Supplier, Satyr Wayfinder, and Mesmeric Orb fill it without tutoring. |
| Reanimate without the commander | Reanimate, Animate Dead, and Victimize let you get threats online before paying five mana or commander tax. |
| Supply power | Lord of Extinction and Consuming Aberration can provide a large pile of counters; Hunted Horror supplies seven power without needing stocked graveyards. |
| Spend the counters | Walking Ballista and Triskelion convert counters directly into damage. | 

([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/the-mimeoplasm))

#### 3. Popular combos

- **Necrotic Ooze + Phyrexian Devourer + Walking Ballista or Triskelion:** With the Ooze on the battlefield and the other two in graveyards, exile cards from your library to add counters, then remove those counters to deal damage. This is **finite**, capped by the total mana value left in your library, but can kill the table. Buried Alive can stock all three; The Mimeoplasm can copy the Ooze by exiling it and an unrelated creature card, leaving both ability donors in the graveyard. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/1656-3693-4899/?utm_source=openai))
- **Mikaeus, the Unhallowed + Triskelion:** With both on the battlefield, use counters to ping opponents and deal enough damage to Triskelion itself to kill it after removing its counters. Undying brings it back and repeats the loop for **infinite damage**. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/combos/the-mimeoplasm?utm_source=openai))

#### 4. Common mistakes

- **Too many power donors, not enough setup.** A hand full of expensive creatures does little without cheap mill, discard, or reanimation. I’d cut Traumatize before cheap setup if the deck already has enough ways to fill its graveyard: it costs five mana and doesn’t affect the battlefield. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/the-mimeoplasm))
- **Expecting both exiled creatures’ abilities.** You copy one creature; the other contributes only its power. Copying Walking Ballista and exiling Phyrexian Devourer does **not** give you the Devourer’s activated ability. You can use opponents’ graveyards, too, so don’t exile your own best reanimation target if an opposing card does the job. ([media.wizards.com](https://media.wizards.com/2022/wpn/marketing_materials/2x2/EN_MTG2X2_Release%20Notes_20220418.pdf?utm_source=openai))

#### 5. Mana base

For a mid-power list, start around **36–37 lands plus 8–10 ramp sources**. Prioritize untapped green for early ramp and early black for graveyard setup, while making sure you have blue by the time you cast your commander. Command Tower and Llanowar Wastes help early; Nature's Lore and Three Visits can fetch Overgrown Tomb or Breeding Pool to fix later colors. Zagoth Triome fixes all three, but don’t overload on tapped lands: the goal is to ramp on turn two and still have mana for setup. Keep basics in ordinary builds; go basic-free only if you deliberately want Hermit Druid to mill your whole library and have a way to win from there. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/the-mimeoplasm))
