# The Mimeoplasm

## Strategy

Build toward turn-six-or-later wins through graveyard-fueled combat, large Mimeoplasm copies, and Living Death rather than an early infect knockout or compact combo. These changes improve early ramp, graveyard setup, repeatable card advantage, and colored mana while preserving meaningful interaction and independent reanimation. Cyclonic Rift, Survival of the Fittest, and Gifts Ungiven occupy the three designated Game Changer slots; Yawgmoth's Will is removed rather than retained alongside them. This is an upper-end Bracket 3 build, with no extra turns, mass land denial, or added infinite combos; its powerful tutor openings should not be used to pursue pre-turn-six eliminations.

**Themes:** Self-mill, Reanimator, Graveyard toolbox, Combat-based Mimeoplasm, Large-creature sacrifice value

## Synergies

- **Build Your Own Giant** (Buried Alive + The Mimeoplasm + Lord of Extinction + Titanoth Rex): Buried Alive puts both creatures into your graveyard, letting The Mimeoplasm copy Titanoth Rex and use Lord of Extinction's graveyard-scaled power for additional +1/+1 counters. The result is an enormous trampling commander that can threaten lethal commander damage.
- **Unblockable Commander Clock** (The Mimeoplasm + Whispersilk Cloak): Whispersilk Cloak makes a counter-loaded The Mimeoplasm unblockable while protecting it from targeted removal. This turns the commander's huge size into a reliable commander-damage threat rather than something opponents can repeatedly chump-block.
- **Tutor Into Teamwide Flying** (Survival of the Fittest + Wonder + Breeding Pool + The Mimeoplasm): Survival of the Fittest can find Wonder, then discard it to another activation while tutoring a useful creature. With Breeding Pool providing the Island subtype, leaving Wonder in your graveyard gives The Mimeoplasm and your other creatures flying.
- **Graveyard-Sized Table Drain** (Lord of Extinction + Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord): Lord of Extinction turns the deck's graveyard filling into enormous power, and Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord converts that power into life loss for every opponent. This provides a potentially lethal finish without needing to attack.
- **Cash Out, Dig Deep** (The Mimeoplasm + Greater Good + Underrealm Lich): Greater Good sacrifices a huge The Mimeoplasm to generate cards equal to its power, then discard three cards. Underrealm Lich replaces each draw with selecting one of three cards and putting the other two into your graveyard, producing both exceptional selection and a massive refill of reanimation targets.
- **Wipe, Then Recruit** (Life's Finale + Sepulchral Primordial): Life's Finale destroys the creature board and stocks one opponent's graveyard with up to three creatures chosen from their library. A subsequent Sepulchral Primordial can steal one of those curated targets and creatures from the other opponents' newly stocked graveyards.
- **Turn One Body Into Two** (Victimize + Yavimaya Elder + Solemn Simulacrum): Sacrificing Yavimaya Elder to Victimize triggers its land-search ability while returning two creatures from your graveyard. Returning Solemn Simulacrum as one of them adds another land to the battlefield and leaves behind a creature that replaces itself when it dies.
- **Renewable Shroud Fuel** (Sylvan Safekeeper + Life from the Loam + Verdant Catacombs): Sylvan Safekeeper turns lands into protection for The Mimeoplasm or another key creature, while Life from the Loam returns those sacrificed lands to your hand. Verdant Catacombs is another reusable land target, and dredging Life from the Loam also advances the graveyard plan.
- **Windfall Feeds the Monsters** (Windfall + Vulturous Zombie + Consuming Aberration): Casting Windfall triggers Consuming Aberration's opponent-milling ability, then resolving it puts opponents' discarded hands into their graveyards. Those cards enlarge Consuming Aberration and give Vulturous Zombie a +1/+1 counter for each card entering an opponent's graveyard.
- **Low-Cost Dredge Reload** (Frantic Search + Golgari Grave-Troll): With Golgari Grave-Troll in your graveyard, replace a Frantic Search draw with dredge 6, then discard the returned Troll during the spell's discard step to prepare another dredge. Untapping up to three lands keeps mana available while you load the graveyard for The Mimeoplasm and reanimation.

## 1. Main strategies/archetypes

- **Self-mill reanimator/toolbox:** fill your graveyard, reanimate valuable creatures, and use The Mimeoplasm as a flexible threat. This is the central strategy; mill, graveyard, and dredge builds overlap heavily.
- **Voltron/infect:** copy an evasive or infect creature, using another creature’s enormous power to supply +1/+1 counters.
- **Graveyard combo:** assemble Necrotic Ooze or reanimation-based combo packages.
- **Secondary themes:** +1/+1 counters and Ooze typal—both represented, but not necessary to the commander’s core plan. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/the-mimeoplasm))

## 4. Common mistakes / underperformers

- **Treating The Mimeoplasm as repeatable reanimation:** it **exiles both cards**. Preserve essential combo pieces, and run separate reanimation spells. It copies only **one** creature’s abilities; the second supplies counters, not keywords. ([media.wizards.com](https://media.wizards.com/2022/wpn/marketing_materials/2x2/EN_MTG2X2_Release%20Notes_20220418.pdf))
- **Keeping outdated, low-impact creatures:** a February 2026 precon rebuild specifically cuts or criticizes **Dark Hatchling**, **Slipstream Eel**, and **Triskelavus**. **Grave Pact** also contributed little without sufficient sacrifice support. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/articles/devour-for-power-commander-precon-remastered?utm_source=openai))
- **Dredging without enough actual draw:** **Golgari Grave-Troll** is powerful, but one budget analysis cut it because the list could not support consistent dredging. That is a build-dependent warning, not a universal cut. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/articles/from-the-brim-to-the-trim-melting-opponents-faces-with-a-budget-reanimator-deck))
- **Assuming raw size is enough:** giant creatures still need evasion; **Jumbo Cactuar**, for example, requires additional help to push damage through. ([cloudflare.edhrec.com](https://cloudflare.edhrec.com/articles/slimer-voracious-apparition-commander-deck-tech?utm_source=openai))

## 5. Mana base advice specific to The Mimeoplasm

- **Practical starting point: 36–38 lands**, then tune downward only with a genuinely low curve and substantial acceleration. Prioritize early **green**, while reliably assembling **{2}{B}{G}{U}**; the 2026 rebuild uses 37 lands and favors land ramp. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/articles/devour-for-power-commander-precon-remastered?utm_source=openai))
- Favor **Command Tower**, **Exotic Orchard**, and efficient colored lands such as **Llanowar Wastes**, **Yavimaya Coast**, and **Underground River** over excessive slow or colorless utility lands. **Cephalid Coliseum** is particularly synergistic as a draw/discard outlet. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/articles/devour-for-power-commander-precon-remastered?utm_source=openai))
- **Choose the Hermit Druid plan before building the lands.** Normal reanimator lists can retain basics and use **Cultivate**, **Kodama's Reach**, and **Sakura-Tribe Elder**; the deterministic Oracle combo instead requires no basics remaining in the library. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/articles/devour-for-power-commander-precon-remastered?utm_source=openai))
