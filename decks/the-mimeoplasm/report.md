### Synergies

- **Unblockable Commander Clock** (The Mimeoplasm + Whispersilk Cloak): Whispersilk Cloak makes a counter-loaded The Mimeoplasm unblockable while protecting it from targeted removal. This turns the commander's huge size into a reliable commander-damage threat rather than something opponents can repeatedly chump-block.
- **Graveyard-Sized Table Drain** (Lord of Extinction + Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord): Lord of Extinction turns the deck's graveyard filling into enormous power, and Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord converts that power into life loss for every opponent. This provides a potentially lethal finish without needing to attack.
- **Wipe, Then Recruit** (Life's Finale + Sepulchral Primordial): Life's Finale destroys the creature board and stocks one opponent's graveyard with up to three creatures chosen from their library. A subsequent Sepulchral Primordial can steal one of those curated targets and creatures from the other opponents' newly stocked graveyards.
- **One-Hit Poison Commander** (Buried Alive + The Mimeoplasm + Skithiryx, the Blight Dragon + Lord of Extinction): Buried Alive puts both creatures into your graveyard, letting The Mimeoplasm copy Skithiryx, the Blight Dragon and gain counters equal to Lord of Extinction's power. If that power is at least 6, the resulting flying infect commander can deliver lethal poison in one unblocked hit, with {B} granting haste.
- **Salvage Into Cheap Reanimation** (Grisly Salvage + Stitch Together): Grisly Salvage can put a reanimation target into your graveyard while keeping a useful creature or land in hand. Its discarded cards, plus Grisly Salvage itself, help reach the seven-card threshold that turns Stitch Together into a two-mana battlefield reanimation spell.
- **Wipe, Draw, Reanimate Their Best** (Decree of Pain + Sepulchral Primordial): Decree of Pain clears creatures into graveyards while drawing you a fresh hand. Follow it with Sepulchral Primordial to reanimate up to one freshly destroyed creature from each opponent's graveyard and rebuild your board with their best threats.
- **Turn the Wipe Into a Dredge Reload** (Decree of Pain + Golgari Grave-Troll + The Mimeoplasm): If Decree of Pain destroys Golgari Grave-Troll, the Troll is in your graveyard before the spell's draws happen, so you can replace one of those draws with dredge 6. The wipe and dredge together stock graveyards with creature choices and counter fuel for The Mimeoplasm while the remaining draws refill your hand.
- **Basic Island, Airborne Ooze** (Island + Wonder + The Mimeoplasm): With Wonder in your graveyard, Island gives your creatures flying, helping a large The Mimeoplasm connect for commander damage. Leave Wonder in the graveyard rather than exiling it to The Mimeoplasm so the evasion stays active.

### Strategy

Build toward turn-six-or-later wins through graveyard-fueled combat, large Mimeoplasm copies, and Living Death rather than an early infect knockout or compact combo. These changes improve early ramp, graveyard setup, repeatable card advantage, and colored mana while preserving meaningful interaction and independent reanimation. Survival of the Fittest and Gifts Ungiven occupy two of the three designated Game Changer slots; Yawgmoth's Will is removed rather than retained alongside them. This is an upper-end Bracket 3 build, with no extra turns, mass land denial, or added infinite combos; its powerful tutor openings should not be used to pursue pre-turn-six eliminations.

### Synergies the swaps add

- **Build Your Own Giant** (Buried Alive + The Mimeoplasm + Lord of Extinction + Titanoth Rex): Buried Alive puts both creatures into your graveyard, letting The Mimeoplasm copy Titanoth Rex and use Lord of Extinction's graveyard-scaled power for additional +1/+1 counters. The result is an enormous trampling commander that can threaten lethal commander damage.
- **Tutor Into Teamwide Flying** (Survival of the Fittest + Wonder + Breeding Pool + The Mimeoplasm): Survival of the Fittest can find Wonder, then discard it to another activation while tutoring a useful creature. With Breeding Pool providing the Island subtype, leaving Wonder in your graveyard gives The Mimeoplasm and your other creatures flying.
- **Turn One Body Into Two** (Victimize + Yavimaya Elder + Solemn Simulacrum): Sacrificing Yavimaya Elder to Victimize triggers its land-search ability while returning two creatures from your graveyard. Returning Solemn Simulacrum as one of them adds another land to the battlefield and leaves behind a creature that replaces itself when it dies.
- **Renewable Shroud Fuel** (Sylvan Safekeeper + Life from the Loam + Verdant Catacombs): Sylvan Safekeeper turns lands into protection for The Mimeoplasm or another key creature, while Life from the Loam returns those sacrificed lands to your hand. Verdant Catacombs is another reusable land target, and dredging Life from the Loam also advances the graveyard plan.
- **Low-Cost Dredge Reload** (Frantic Search + Golgari Grave-Troll): With Golgari Grave-Troll in your graveyard, replace a Frantic Search draw with dredge 6, then discard the returned Troll during the spell's discard step to prepare another dredge. Untapping up to three lands keeps mana available while you load the graveyard for The Mimeoplasm and reanimation.
- **Loot Into an Infect Dragon** (Likeness Looter + Skithiryx, the Blight Dragon): Likeness Looter can discard Skithiryx, the Blight Dragon while filtering your hand, then pay {5} to become its copy without removing it from your graveyard. The copy gains infect and regeneration, and another {B} grants haste so it can attack immediately.
- **Buried Dragon, Immediate Threat** (Entomb + Necromancy + Skithiryx, the Blight Dragon): Entomb finds Skithiryx, the Blight Dragon for Necromancy to reanimate, bypassing its normal casting cost. Reanimate it during your precombat main phase and pay {B} for haste to immediately threaten four poison counters through the air.
- **Sacrifice, Dredge, Reload** (Golgari Grave-Troll + Greater Good): Sacrifice a large Golgari Grave-Troll to Greater Good, then replace one of the resulting draws with its dredge 6 to return it to your hand. The remaining draws and subsequent discards stock your hand and graveyard, while the milled creatures can make the next Grave-Troll larger.
- **Fetch the Flying Switch** (Farseek + Watery Grave + Wonder): With Wonder in your graveyard, Farseek can find Watery Grave because it is an Island and a Swamp. Watery Grave enables Wonder's teamwide flying even while tapped, helping your oversized graveyard-powered creatures connect.

## Background

### Web research about the commander

#### 1. Main strategies/archetypes

- **Self-mill reanimator/toolbox:** fill your graveyard, reanimate valuable creatures, and use The Mimeoplasm as a flexible threat. This is the central strategy; mill, graveyard, and dredge builds overlap heavily.
- **Voltron/infect:** copy an evasive or infect creature, using another creature’s enormous power to supply +1/+1 counters.
- **Graveyard combo:** assemble Necrotic Ooze or reanimation-based combo packages.
- **Secondary themes:** +1/+1 counters and Ooze typal—both represented, but not necessary to the commander’s core plan. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/the-mimeoplasm))

#### 2. Key synergy cards and engines

| Role | Specific cards |
|---|---|
| **Find and stock the graveyard** | **Entomb**, **Buried Alive**, **Survival of the Fittest**, **Fauna Shaman** |
| **Mill while improving your hand** | **Grisly Salvage**, **Frantic Search**, **Fact or Fiction**, **Stitcher's Supplier**, **Mesmeric Orb** |
| **Independent reanimation** | **Reanimate**, **Animate Dead**, **Necromancy**, **Victimize**, **Living Death**—important so the deck does not depend entirely on its commander |
| **Large counter donors** | **Lord of Extinction**, **Consuming Aberration**, **Yargle and Multani**, **Death's Shadow** |
| **Useful copy targets** | **Skithiryx, the Blight Dragon**, **Blighted Agent**, **Titanoth Rex**, **Woodfall Primus** |
| **Long-game resources** | **Life from the Loam**, **Muldrotha, the Gravetide**, **Underrealm Lich** |

These recur across current EDHREC recommendations and dedicated deckbuilding articles; select targets according to your chosen win condition rather than including every package. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/the-mimeoplasm?utm_source=openai))

#### 3. Popular combos and finishing combinations

| Exact cards | Setup and result |
|---|---|
| **Necrotic Ooze + Phyrexian Devourer + Triskelion** | Ooze on the battlefield; the other two in graveyards. Exile library cards to put counters on Ooze, then remove counters to deal damage. **Potentially table-lethal, but finite**, limited by the remaining library’s total mana value—not genuinely infinite. |
| **Mikaeus, the Unhallowed + Triskelion** | Both on the battlefield. Remove counters for damage, reserving two shots to kill Triskelion itself; undying returns it. **Infinite damage and death/enter/leave triggers.** |
| **Kederekt Leviathan + Animate Dead** | Reanimate Leviathan; its trigger returns other nonland permanents, including Animate Dead, to hand, causing Leviathan’s sacrifice. **Repeatable nonland-board reset for {1}{B} each iteration**, not a free infinite loop. |
| **Hermit Druid + Thassa's Oracle** | With **no basic lands in your library**, an active Druid mills everything for {G}; cast Oracle from hand for {U}{U}. **Wins the game.** |

Commander Spellbook documents these sequences and their prerequisites. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/1495-1656-4899/?q=card:%22Triskelion%22))

**Commander-specific finisher:** The Mimeoplasm copying **Blighted Agent**, with **Yargle and Multani** supplying 18 counters, becomes a **19/19 unblockable infect creature**—lethal to one opponent if it connects, but it still needs haste or a turn cycle. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/articles/slimer-voracious-apparition-commander-deck-tech?utm_source=openai))

#### 4. Common mistakes / underperformers

- **Treating The Mimeoplasm as repeatable reanimation:** it **exiles both cards**. Preserve essential combo pieces, and run separate reanimation spells. It copies only **one** creature’s abilities; the second supplies counters, not keywords. ([media.wizards.com](https://media.wizards.com/2022/wpn/marketing_materials/2x2/EN_MTG2X2_Release%20Notes_20220418.pdf))
- **Keeping outdated, low-impact creatures:** a February 2026 precon rebuild specifically cuts or criticizes **Dark Hatchling**, **Slipstream Eel**, and **Triskelavus**. **Grave Pact** also contributed little without sufficient sacrifice support. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/articles/devour-for-power-commander-precon-remastered?utm_source=openai))
- **Dredging without enough actual draw:** **Golgari Grave-Troll** is powerful, but one budget analysis cut it because the list could not support consistent dredging. That is a build-dependent warning, not a universal cut. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/articles/from-the-brim-to-the-trim-melting-opponents-faces-with-a-budget-reanimator-deck))
- **Assuming raw size is enough:** giant creatures still need evasion; **Jumbo Cactuar**, for example, requires additional help to push damage through. ([cloudflare.edhrec.com](https://cloudflare.edhrec.com/articles/slimer-voracious-apparition-commander-deck-tech?utm_source=openai))

#### 5. Mana base advice specific to The Mimeoplasm

- **Practical starting point: 36–38 lands**, then tune downward only with a genuinely low curve and substantial acceleration. Prioritize early **green**, while reliably assembling **{2}{B}{G}{U}**; the 2026 rebuild uses 37 lands and favors land ramp. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/articles/devour-for-power-commander-precon-remastered?utm_source=openai))
- Favor **Command Tower**, **Exotic Orchard**, and efficient colored lands such as **Llanowar Wastes**, **Yavimaya Coast**, and **Underground River** over excessive slow or colorless utility lands. **Cephalid Coliseum** is particularly synergistic as a draw/discard outlet. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/articles/devour-for-power-commander-precon-remastered?utm_source=openai))
- **Choose the Hermit Druid plan before building the lands.** Normal reanimator lists can retain basics and use **Cultivate**, **Kodama's Reach**, and **Sakura-Tribe Elder**; the deterministic Oracle combo instead requires no basics remaining in the library. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/articles/devour-for-power-commander-precon-remastered?utm_source=openai))
