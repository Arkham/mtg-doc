## Strategy

Build toward Derevi stax-combo: develop efficient mana creatures, connect in combat, and use Derevi's untaps to operate through Winter Orb, Hokori, and Rising Waters. These changes replace expensive reactive cards with mana engines, premium draw, protection, tutors, and a decisive infinite-mana payoff while removing artifact taxes that undermine your own engines. This is a substantial move toward upper Bracket 4 within the 15-swap limit, not a fully rebuilt competitive list; play it with the stated expectation of no wins or effectively game-ending locks before turn four.

**Themes:** Stax and asymmetric untapping, Creature-based ramp, Blink and enters-the-battlefield engines, Infinite mana, Combat-triggered value, Tutor-backed combo finishes

## Synergies

- **Break Winter Orb's Symmetry** (Derevi, Empyrial Tactician + Winter Orb + Opposition): Opposition can tap Winter Orb during the end step before your turn, letting all your lands untap before the Orb resumes restricting opponents. Derevi supplies additional untaps for your resources, while Opposition can tap opponents' available lands during their upkeep.
- **Mana Under the Lock** (Derevi, Empyrial Tactician + Hokori, Dust Drinker + Bloom Tender): Derevi supplies all three colors for Bloom Tender to produce {G}{W}{U}, and each combat-damage trigger can untap the Tender for another activation. This gives you substantial mana while Hokori, Dust Drinker keeps opponents' lands largely frozen.
- **Evasive Combat Pays Twice** (Derevi, Empyrial Tactician + Bident of Thassa + Lu Xun, Scholar General): Lu Xun, Scholar General's horsemanship makes it a reliable attacker, and connecting draws one card from its own ability plus another from Bident of Thassa. The same hit also triggers Derevi to untap a mana source or utility permanent.
- **Accelerated Ring Draws** (Derevi, Empyrial Tactician + The One Ring): Derevi's combat-damage triggers can repeatedly untap The One Ring, allowing multiple increasingly large draw activations in one combat. Each activation adds another burden counter, so the extra cards come with a growing upkeep-life cost.
- **Wizard Assembly Line** (Derevi, Empyrial Tactician + Azami, Lady of Scrolls + Stonybrook Schoolmaster): Azami can tap Stonybrook Schoolmaster to draw a card and create a Merfolk Wizard token, then tap that new token to draw another card immediately. Derevi's triggers can untap the Schoolmaster to repeat the process, or untap other Wizards for more draws.
- **Every-Turn Token Lock** (Seedborn Muse + Squirrel Nest + Opposition): Seedborn Muse untaps the enchanted land and your creatures during every opponent's untap step, letting Squirrel Nest produce another token each turn. Opposition turns those tokens into repeatable control over opposing lands, creatures, and artifacts.
- **Selective Untap Advantage** (Derevi, Empyrial Tactician + Stoic Angel + Murkfiend Liege): Stoic Angel limits each player's normal creature untaps, but Murkfiend Liege refreshes your green and blue creatures during opponents' untap steps. Derevi adds targeted untaps outside the untap step, helping your board keep attacking and activating abilities while opponents struggle to reset.
- **Blink Into Removal and Recursion** (Emiel the Blessed + Aura Shards + Eternal Witness): Each activation of Emiel the Blessed blinking Eternal Witness returns a card from your graveyard and triggers Aura Shards to destroy an artifact or enchantment. This converts spare mana into repeatable recursion and board control without requiring an infinite loop.
- **Renewable Counterspell Fodder** (Derevi, Empyrial Tactician + Voidmage Prodigy): Derevi is a Wizard, so Voidmage Prodigy can sacrifice it to counter any spell. Returning Derevi to the command zone lets you deploy it again for {1}{G}{W}{U}, bypassing commander tax and gaining another enters-the-battlefield tap or untap trigger.
- **Squirrels Become a Win Condition** (Squirrel Nest + Mirror Entity + Derevi, Empyrial Tactician): Derevi can untap the enchanted land to generate additional Squirrels, building a board without committing more creature cards. Mirror Entity turns those small tokens into large attackers, and each creature that connects generates another Derevi trigger.

([cloudflare.edhrec.com](https://cloudflare.edhrec.com/articles/power-sink-derevis-zombie-apocalypse))
- **Creature-toolbox/blink combo:** **Birthing Pod**, **Yisan, the Wanderer Bard**, and creature tutors assemble repeatable Derevi enter-the-battlefield loops. ([tappedout.net](https://tappedout.net/mtg-decks/bird-person-primer/))
- **Evasive combat/Bird typal:** Flyers generate many combat-damage triggers; **Kastral, the Windcrested** and Bird payoffs turn that board into cards and damage. ([cloudflare.edhrec.com](https://cloudflare.edhrec.com/articles/technically-playable-derevi-empyrial-tactician))
- **Tap/untap value:** Repeatedly activate powerful mana producers and card-draw permanents rather than committing to a hard lock. ([cloudflare.edhrec.com](https://cloudflare.edhrec.com/articles/technically-playable-derevi-empyrial-tactician))

## 2. Key synergy cards and engines

- **Mana:** **Bloom Tender**, **Faeburrow Elder**, **Circle of Dreams Druid**, and **Gaea's Cradle** reward concentrating Derevi’s untaps on one high-output permanent. **Wild Growth** and **Utopia Sprawl** similarly improve each land untap. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/derevi-empyrial-tactician))
- **Cards:** **The One Ring** can be activated between successive untap triggers. **Edric, Spymaster of Trest**, **Toski, Bearer of Secrets**, and **Bident of Thassa** reward the same combat connections Derevi already wants. ([cloudflare.edhrec.com](https://cloudflare.edhrec.com/articles/technically-playable-derevi-empyrial-tactician))
- **Control:** **Verity Circle** turns Derevi tapping opposing creatures into draws; **Willbreaker** turns targeting opposing creatures into theft. **Aura Shards** converts creature entries—including repeated Derevi entries—into artifact/enchantment removal. ([cloudflare.edhrec.com](https://cloudflare.edhrec.com/articles/power-sink-derevis-zombie-apocalypse))
- **Toolbox:** **Birthing Pod**, **Yisan, the Wanderer Bard**, **Eladamri's Call**, and **Chord of Calling** help find the appropriate engine or combo creature. ([tappedout.net](https://tappedout.net/mtg-decks/bird-person-primer/))

## 4. Common mistakes/underperformers

- **Mixing every archetype without a finish.** EDHREC’s precon analysis specifically flags lack of focus and closing power. Decide whether your principal finish is combat or combo. ([cloudflare.edhrec.com](https://cloudflare.edhrec.com/articles/technically-playable-derevi-empyrial-tactician))
- **Assuming every blink loop makes mana.** The Bloom Tender and three-creature Gaea's Cradle loops above are mana-neutral without additional help. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/combo/2608-4499-5168/))
- **Overloading on slow lands.** One older pilot primer specifically reports tapped lands, **Cloud of Faeries** outside combos, and **Slithermuse** underperforming. Treat those creature cuts as build-specific experience, not universal consensus. ([tappedout.net](https://tappedout.net/mtg-decks/derevi-empyrial-tactician-edh-primer/))
- **Making combat mana without a combat-speed outlet.** Untap-trigger mana needs to be used before the step ends; it does not automatically fund second-main-phase spells. Also, repeated **The One Ring** activations require a plan for its growing burden counters. ([cloudflare.edhrec.com](https://cloudflare.edhrec.com/articles/technically-playable-derevi-empyrial-tactician))
- **Copying obsolete lists:** **Nadu, Winged Wisdom**, **Mana Crypt**, and **Jeweled Lotus** remain banned in Commander. ([magic.wizards.com](https://magic.wizards.com/en/banned-restricted-list?utm_source=openai))

## 5. Mana base advice specific to Derevi

- **Prioritize early green and access to all three colors.** Derevi costs **{G}{W}{U}** to cast; its command-zone activation costs **{1}{G}{W}{U}**. Untaps cannot fix a missing color. ([commanderspellbook.com](https://commanderspellbook.com/card/2608/))
- **Favor untapped fixing:** **Command Tower**, **Breeding Pool**, **Temple Garden**, **Hallowed Fountain**, fetch lands, and—at lower budgets—**Yavimaya Coast**, **Brushland**, and **Adarkar Wastes**. Multiplayer options include **Rejuvenating Springs**, **Bountiful Promenade**, and **Sea of Clouds**. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/derevi-empyrial-tactician))
- **Balance output against tempo:** **Gaea's Cradle** is an exceptional untap target. **Lotus Field** and bounce lands provide multiple mana per untap but impose real setup costs; don’t fill the deck with them merely because Derevi can untap them. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/derevi-empyrial-tactician))
- **My practical starting point:** around **35–36 lands for ordinary casual builds**, then adjust through testing. Don’t copy an aggressively low land count from a specialized combo list without also copying—and understanding—its acceleration package.
