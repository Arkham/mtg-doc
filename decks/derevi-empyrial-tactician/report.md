### How to play

Derevi, Empyrial Tactician turns combat hits into extra mana and helps break parity under Winter Orb. Keep opponents short on mana, then finish with a wide attack or repeated combat steps under the lock.

**Early game**

- Use Cultivate or Kodama's Reach to get your missing colors.
- Land Derevi, Empyrial Tactician early and use its entrance trigger to untap a land.
- Untap Azorius Chancery after a combat hit to reuse both mana.

**Mid game**

- Attack with Lu Xun, Scholar General alongside Bident of Thassa to draw two cards.
- Use Opposition to tap opposing lands during upkeep and blockers before combat.
- Tap Winter Orb with Opposition before your untap step so your lands untap normally.

**Late game**

- Untap the land enchanted by Squirrel Nest to make more tokens for Aura Shards.
- Stack zero-value activations of Mirror Entity and recycle Reveillark with Karmic Guide.
- Return Acidic Slime alongside Karmic Guide to destroy another land each loop.

**How it wins**

- Pump your tokens with Mirror Entity and swing after Opposition taps their blockers.
- Land True Conviction to double combat damage and get twice the triggers from Derevi, Empyrial Tactician.
- Loop Acidic Slime with Reveillark to dismantle opposing mana, then win through combat.

**Key cards**

- Derevi, Empyrial Tactician: Turns combat hits into mana or taps down blockers.
- Opposition: Turns spare creatures into repeatable mana denial.
- Winter Orb: Restricts land untaps only while it stays untapped.
- Stonybrook Schoolmaster: Makes a token whenever it becomes tapped.
- Murkfiend Liege: Readies blue and green creatures on opponents' turns.

**Mulligan:** Keep three lands with access to all three colors, plus Cultivate or an early play like Rhystic Study.

**Watch out for**

- Avoid playing Winter Orb before you can tap it or untap extra lands.
- Graveyard exile can break the loop by removing Karmic Guide before Reveillark returns it.
- Sunblast Angel destroys your tapped attackers too, so check your board before casting it.

### Synergies

- **One-Sided Winter** (Derevi, Empyrial Tactician + Opposition + Winter Orb): Use Opposition to tap Winter Orb during the end step before your turn so all your lands can untap. Then use a combat-damage trigger from Derevi, Empyrial Tactician to untap Winter Orb before your opponents' turns.
- **Tap, Token, Repeat** (Derevi, Empyrial Tactician + Stonybrook Schoolmaster + Opposition + Murkfiend Liege): Tap Stonybrook Schoolmaster with Opposition to deny an opposing permanent and create a token that can immediately pay for another activation. Derevi, Empyrial Tactician untaps the Schoolmaster when your creatures connect, and Murkfiend Liege refreshes it and its blue tokens during each opponent's untap step.
- **Untap and Tax** (Derevi, Empyrial Tactician + Stoic Angel + Windborn Muse + Rising Waters): Stoic Angel and Rising Waters restrict normal creature and land untaps, but Derevi, Empyrial Tactician lets successful attacks untap your creatures or mana sources outside the untap step. Windborn Muse makes it harder for opponents to attack you with their limited mana.
- **Nest-Fueled Demolition** (Derevi, Empyrial Tactician + Squirrel Nest + Aura Shards): Use triggers from Derevi, Empyrial Tactician to untap the land enchanted by Squirrel Nest, turning each untap into another Squirrel. Each token entering gives Aura Shards another chance to destroy an artifact or enchantment.
- **Hit for Cards and Mana** (Derevi, Empyrial Tactician + Bident of Thassa + Lu Xun, Scholar General): When Lu Xun, Scholar General connects through horsemanship, it can draw a card on its own and another through Bident of Thassa, while Derevi, Empyrial Tactician can untap a mana source to help cast what you drew.

### Strategy

Accelerate into Derevi, Empyrial Tactician, then turn combat damage into extra mana and use her triggers to break parity on Winter Orb. Cheap draw engines and tutors help you assemble Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy plus Basalt Monolith for unlimited colorless mana, which Finale of Devastation can turn into a lethal attack if you have the green mana and creatures to connect. This is optimized Bracket 4 stax-combo: spend the opening turns building mana, drawing cards, and disrupting opponents, with turn four or later as the expected closing window.

### How to play the suggested deck

Derevi, Empyrial Tactician turns combat damage into extra mana while Opposition keeps opponents short on resources. Finish with a huge Finale of Devastation or use infinite colorless mana to deck an opponent with Stroke of Genius.

**Early game**

- Lead with Birds of Paradise to fix your colors for Derevi, Empyrial Tactician.
- Play Bloom Tender early so Derevi, Empyrial Tactician lets it tap for three colors.

**Mid game**

- Attack with Lu Xun, Scholar General to draw cards and trigger Derevi, Empyrial Tactician.
- Use Opposition to tap Winter Orb before your untap step so your lands untap normally.
- Tap Stonybrook Schoolmaster to pay for Opposition, then use the new token to tap something.

**Late game**

- Use Enlightened Tutor to find Basalt Monolith when Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy is ready.
- Blink Eternal Witness with Emiel the Blessed to recover a countered Finale of Devastation.

**How it wins**

- Pair Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy with Basalt Monolith to fuel a lethal Stroke of Genius.
- Equip Sword of the Paruns to Bloom Tender producing four mana, then loop untaps.
- Spend that mana on Finale of Devastation to give your creatures lethal power and haste.

**Key cards**

- Derevi, Empyrial Tactician: Untaps mana sources on entry and when your creatures connect.
- Opposition: Turns tokens into repeatable land disruption or clears blockers.
- Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy: Boosts nonland mana and makes Basalt Monolith go infinite.
- Emiel the Blessed: Repeats entry triggers and turns commander untaps into combo loops.

**Mulligan:** Keep a hand with early access to all three colors, acceleration like Birds of Paradise, and a payoff like Bident of Thassa.

**Watch out for**

- Winter Orb slows you too unless you tap it before your turn or have nonland mana.
- Lightning Greaves prevents Emiel the Blessed from targeting the equipped creature.

### Synergies the swaps add

- **Break the Mana Lock** (Derevi, Empyrial Tactician + Hokori, Dust Drinker + Selesnya Sanctuary + Mana Vault): Hokori, Dust Drinker keeps lands from untapping normally, but Derevi, Empyrial Tactician turns combat hits into extra untaps of Selesnya Sanctuary for two mana or Mana Vault for three. The artifact also keeps your mana production from depending entirely on lands.
- **Three Colors, Four Mana** (Derevi, Empyrial Tactician + Bloom Tender + Faeburrow Elder + Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy): Derevi, Empyrial Tactician supplies all three colors for Bloom Tender and Faeburrow Elder, and Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy raises either creature's output to four mana per tap. Combat hits let you untap and reuse those mana creatures to fund spells or more activations of Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy.
- **Blink, Recover, Rebuild** (Emiel the Blessed + Reveillark + Eternal Witness + Sun Titan): Blinking Reveillark with Emiel the Blessed can bring back Eternal Witness to recover any card from your graveyard. Blinking Sun Titan gives you another way to return Eternal Witness or recover a different cheap permanent directly to the battlefield.

### Web research about the commander

#### Derevi, Empyrial Tactician — Commander deckbuilding guide

**1. Main builds**
- **Birds / flying tempo:** Lead with cheap evasive creatures, turn combat damage into extra mana or untaps, and win through the air.
- **Tap/untap value or stax:** Reuse mana sources and tap abilities, or break the symmetry of untap restrictions. Creature-based disruption also gives you bodies to attack with.
- **Blink / combo:** Repeatedly blink your commander to untap a mana source. More competitive lists often add a creature toolbox built around Birthing Pod. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/derevi-empyrial-tactician?utm_source=openai))

**2. Key synergy cards**
- **Combat:** Kastral, the Windcrested rewards Bird hits; Reconnaissance Mission turns connecting creatures into cards.
- **Mana:** Bloom Tender, Faeburrow Elder, and lands enchanted with Utopia Sprawl or Wild Growth make each untap worth more.
- **Stax:** Winter Orb and Static Orb stop restricting untaps while tapped. Use the commander’s entry trigger to tap your own Orb before your turn, then untap normally. Stasis is a different plan: your triggers can help pay its upkeep, but tapping it doesn’t turn it off. ([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/derevi-empyrial-tactician?utm_source=openai))

**3. Established combo lines**

| Cards | What they produce |
|---|---|
| Derevi, Empyrial Tactician + Emiel the Blessed + Bloom Tender | With the mana creature ready to tap for three, blink the commander repeatedly for unlimited entry and exit triggers. **No net mana** at three per tap. |
| Derevi, Empyrial Tactician + Deadeye Navigator + Bloom Tender | Pair the navigator with the commander. Each blink costs two and untaps the mana creature, which taps for three: unlimited green and white mana and entry/exit triggers. |
| Derevi, Empyrial Tactician + Ashnod's Altar + Bloom Tender | Tap the mana creature **before** sacrificing the commander. Return the commander from the command zone and untap the mana creature. Nets one colorless mana per loop, plus unlimited death and entry triggers. |

([edhrec.com](https://edhrec.com/commanders/derevi-empyrial-tactician/core/combos))

**4. Common pitfalls.** Don’t fill a combat build with expensive Birds that can’t help you connect early; Airborne Aid is a cut candidate if you rarely have several Birds on board. Don’t pack untap locks without a way to keep attacking or close the game. And don’t count combat-generated mana toward ordinary postcombat spells: spend it during combat, leave the source untapped for later, or run instant-speed outlets. The command-zone ability isn’t a cast and doesn’t incur commander tax. ([cloudflare.edhrec.com](https://cloudflare.edhrec.com/articles/technically-playable-derevi-empyrial-tactician?utm_source=openai))

**5. Mana base.** Start around **35–37 lands** for a non-cEDH build. Prioritize untapped green on turn one and access to all three colors by turn three. Breeding Pool and Temple Garden fix colors and can carry Utopia Sprawl; keep basic green sources too. Limit tapped lands and colorless utility slots: the command-zone ability still needs green, white, and blue. Gaea's Cradle is a strong optional untap target in creature-heavy lists, not a substitute for reliable opening green. ([cloudflare.edhrec.com](https://cloudflare.edhrec.com/commanders/derevi-empyrial-tactician/core/birds/decks?utm_source=openai))
